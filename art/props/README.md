# Props family (`Prop`)

Terrain dressing and settlement props for DEMIURGE: rocks, boulders and cliff chunks; fences, walls and
hedgerows (tiling); crop rows and hay; barrels, crates, carts and market goods; signposts; one street lamp per
age; statues with damage states. Everything here is generated by the scripts in this folder (original work,
procedural textures, licence `original`); nothing is hand-edited.

Targets: `docs/concept/settlements/` (the river-valley town through the ages, the three invented cultures)
and `docs/concept/nature/` (the descent series and the biomes).

## What is here

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `build_props.py` | Entry point: textures (optional), every mesh with its LODs, bakes, FBX exports, `.blend`, manifest |
| `props_lib.py` | Geometry builder (Parts, lathe, tube, cards, chamfered boxes), UVs, LOD decimation, preview materials, FBX export |
| `props_rocks.py` | Rocks and cliffs: sculpted high-poly, decimated game mesh, Cycles bake of normal and AO |
| `props_kit.py` | Generators for everything else (fences, hedges, crops, hay, storage, carts, market, signs, lamps, statues) |
| `props_models.py` | The registry: asset name, generator, material slots, ages, cultures, states, placement hints |
| `props_tex.py` | Tileable texture sets (numpy; Wood and Straw reuse `art/tools/make_textures.py` oak and thatch) |
| `props_preview.py` | Preview renders through the slot tool (ASSETS.md section 3) |
| `export/SM_Prop_*.fbx` | One FBX per mesh: an FBX LOD group (`fbx_type = LodGroup`) with `_LOD0` .. `_LOD2` (`_LOD3` for cliffs) |
| `textures/T_Prop_*` | Tiling sets `_D` (sRGB), `_N` (OpenGL +Y, flip green in UE), `_ORM` (linear); baked rock maps `T_Prop_RockA_N` etc. |
| `source/props.blend` | Source scene: one collection per mesh, preview node materials on the masters |
| `manifest.json` | Per mesh: footprint, height, depth below ground, triangles per LOD, slots and materials, textures, material instances, ages, cultures, state group, placement, licence, script |

Previews: `shots/asset-props-<scene>-<view>.png`, scenes `rocks`, `farm`, `village`, `lamps`, `statues`,
`night` at `40m`, `300m`, `1km`, plus `lineup-close` (every small prop in rows, for inspection only). Verifier
views: `shots/asset-props-verify-lods-{fields,goods,signs,statues}.png` (each asset with LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 front to
back) and `shots/asset-props-verify-bakes.png` (baked rocks and cliffs close up).

## Conventions used (beyond docs/ASSETS.md)

- **Slots.** At most three, always in the order Structure, Roof, Detail. On props, Roof is simply the second
  material (awning cloth, the lamp's metal head, the hedge tree's wood) and Detail the third (iron hoops,
  produce, and on lamps **always the light**: `M_Prop_Glow`, emissive at night only). The manifest lists
  slot index, role and master material for every mesh.
- **Masters and instances.** `M_Prop_Rock` (baked, see below), `M_Prop_RockTile` (tiling rock: dry-stone wall,
  cairn), `M_Prop_Wood`, `M_Prop_Stone`, `M_Prop_Straw`, `M_Prop_Cloth`, `M_Prop_Metal` (old iron, rusts),
  `M_Prop_Steel` (clean modern metal), `M_Prop_Foliage` and `M_Prop_Crop` (masked, two-sided),
  `M_Prop_Glow`. Culture variation is by material instance tint (plus metallic and roughness scales where
  listed): Wood, Stone and Cloth have `Valley`, `CliffTerrace`, `LagoonWeaver`, `RingBuilder`
  (awnings: madder red, cobalt, saffron, heather); rocks have biome instances `Granite`, `Sandstone`,
  `Basalt`, `Limestone`; crops `Ripe`, `Green`, `Barley`; foliage `Summer`, `Autumn`, `Dry`, `Cabbage`;
  metal `Iron`, `PaintedGreen`, `PaintedBlack`; steel `Brushed`, `Galvanised`, `PaintedWhite`,
  `PaintedGrey`; glow `Fire`, `Oil`, `Gas`, `Sodium`, `LED`. The values are in `manifest.json`
  (`material_instances`).
- **Vertex colour `Col`** (byte, per corner): RGB multiplies the base colour (per-plank and per-stone
  variation, produce and pottery colours, charring on burnt states); A is the moss / grime mask (used by
  `M_Prop_Rock` and `M_Prop_RockTile`: moss where A is high and the surface faces up).
- **UVs.** UV0 `UVMap` is in texture tiles (metres divided by the set's `tile_m`, see `texture_sets` in the
  manifest), so textures tile at a constant texel density. UV1 `UVUnique` is a 0..1 non-overlapping layout
  (lightmaps). **Rocks and cliffs are the exception:** UV0 is `UVUnique` (their baked normal and AO; UE
  builds the tangent basis from UV0) and UV1 is the tiling detail.
- **Rock material in UE:** base = `T_Prop_Rock_D` on UV1 x MI tint x baked AO (`T_Prop_<Asset>_ORM`.R on UV0);
  normal = baked `T_Prop_<Asset>_N` (UV0) blended with the detail normal (world-aligned or UV1); moss =
  `T_Prop_Moss_*` where vertex A x up-facing is high.
- **Tiling props** (fences, walls, hedges, crops) are 4.0 m along local X with the pivot at the tile centre;
  end posts of neighbouring segments coincide. Crops tile 4 x 4 m; vine rows go 2 m apart.
- **Ground.** Pivots at the footprint centre on Z = 0; posts, plinths and rocks extend 0.2-0.4 m below
  (cliffs 1-2 m) so they sit on slopes (`below_ground_m` in the manifest).
- **States.** Fences `_Damaged`; market stall `_Damaged`, `_Burnt`; statues `_Damaged`, `_Ruin`. All states
  share the footprint; the manifest groups them (`variant_group`, `state`, `states`).

## How to regenerate

From the project root (every Blender run that renders or bakes goes through the slot tool):

```
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/props/build_props.py -- --textures
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b art/props/source/props.blend --python art/props/props_preview.py -- farm 40m 300m 1km
```

`--only Rock_A,Lamp_Age4` rebuilds single assets and keeps the rest of the `.blend` and manifest;
`--nobake` skips the Cycles bakes. A full build takes about 2 minutes on an idle machine (most of it the 12
bakes). Preview scenes: `rocks farm village lamps statues night lineup`; views `close 40m 300m 1km`. Verifier
scenes (view `close`): `lods-fields lods-goods lods-signs lods-statues lods-rocks lods-cliffs bakes`.

## Budgets

All props are within 50-1k triangles at LOD0 except, deliberately:
- **Cliff chunks (2,000):** terrain-scale pieces 10-22 m across, closer to a house than a prop; they carry a
  baked normal map and have a LOD3 hull (70-260 triangles).
- **Cart_Wagon (2,052):** a vehicle (1-4k budget).
- **Fence_DryStone (1,128), Market_Stall_Damaged (1,240), Goods_Pots (1,100), Crop_Veg (1,008):** slightly
  over 1k; they are 4 m tiles or composite props.
- LOD1 / LOD2 aim at 40 % / 12 % but never at the cost of the silhouette (see Verification): thin props made of
  posts and wheels keep more (Cart_Hand 548 / 484, Lamp_Age2-4 and Fence_Rail repeat LOD1 as LOD2), the dry-stone
  wall drops to a 28-triangle wall hull at LOD2. Exact counts in the manifest; no LOD is heavier than the one before.

## Known gaps

- Hedge, crop and vine foliage are alpha cards on a solid core; up close (under about 15 m) the cards read as
  cards. The hedge tree is a small lobed crown, not a real tree asset (the tree family owns those).
- Glow is a flat emissive material: lamps need a real light (or the town lights system) to light the ground.
- No `_Construction` states (props are not built in stages); no `_Drowned` variants.
- Blank boards on signs (concept rule: no text); no culture-specific mesh variants yet, only material
  instances (e.g. Lagoon-Weaver tarred piles or Ring-Builder stone posts would be new meshes).
- The FBX LOD group is written with Blender's `fbx_type = LodGroup` custom property; import with
  "Import Mesh LODs" on. Screen sizes are in the manifest, not in the FBX.
- Textures are 1024 tiling sets plus 12 baked 1024 rock sets; no trim sheet atlas yet.
- Fence tiles carry a post at both ends, so neighbouring tiles put two identical-size posts in the same place
  (different vertex tint and UV offset): expect slight z-fighting on end posts up close. A cleaner kit would own
  one end post per tile plus a separate end post.
- With the ASSETS.md screen sizes (0.35 / 0.12) a 4 m prop switches to LOD1 at about 10 m and LOD2 at about
  25-30 m, so the game camera (40 m and up) mostly sees LOD1 / LOD2 of small props, not LOD0 as the preview
  scenes assume. The integration agent may want larger screen sizes for props.
- Cliff_B LOD3 is 210 triangles (the collapse decimator cannot go lower without folding the columns); the
  others are 68-70.
- The rock and cliff normal bakes have a few inverted texels (tangent Z < 0): 6-12 % of the texels on Rock_F,
  Rock_H and Cliff_B, but they sit in the deep crevices between stones and columns (AO < 0.2) or below ground;
  under 1 % of the lit surface.

## Verification (independent verifier, 2026-11-19)

Rebuilt from a clean `build_props.py -- --textures` run (reproducible: the manifest is identical between runs and
the FBX files differ only in their embedded timestamps), re-imported all 65 FBX files and all 199 LOD meshes in
Blender and rendered the verifier views above. Before the fixes the build was reproducible as delivered, except
`T_Prop_Straw_D`, which differs by up to 6/255 because the shared `art/tools/make_textures.py` changed after the
builder's run. Fixes made here (all in art/props/):

1. **LODs folded into sheets.** Collapse-decimating LOD0 straight to 12 % folded small closed parts (boxes,
   stones, posts, the hay rick cone) into zero-volume pairs of duplicate faces: 53 of the 199 LOD meshes had them. The FBX
   importer drops those faces, so the manifest triangle counts did not match the FBX for 34 meshes. The worst cases
   were the Fence_DryStone LOD2, where all 94 stones had become flaps; the Hay_Stack LOD2, where only the pole and slivers
   were left; and Crate_Stack LOD2, where the crates were sheets. `props_models.make` now builds LOD k from `gen(k)`
   and decimates only when needed. It accepts a decimation only if it keeps the bounding box (90 %), the surface
   area (90 %) and has under 12 % folded faces, and otherwise keeps the undecimated or previous LOD.
   `props_lib.clean_mesh` deletes duplicate faces and loose vertices after every decimation. Native LOD2s were
   added for the dry-stone wall (a battered wall hull), crates (plain boxes) and the hay rick (fewer rings, no pole).
2. **Loose vertices** were exported in 12 LOD meshes: decimated LOD2s, and the torn awnings of Market_Stall_Damaged and
   _Burnt at every LOD. `build_object` and `clean_mesh` now delete them.
3. **Cliff_B LOD3 equalled LOD2** (256 = 256). LODs are now decimated in a chain, so it is 210.
4. **Rock fissures read as inked outlines.** Zero-crossing noise made closed cells of 0.4-0.8 m deep grooves (a
   "giraffe" pattern on the cliffs at 40 m). The cliffs now have mostly vertical joints, half as deep and narrower,
   and each rock's fissures follow one joint direction. All 12 maps were re-baked.
5. **Crop_Vine canopy** was one ellipsoid tapering to nothing at the tile ends, so tiled rows read as sausage
   links every 4 m. It is now a continuous leafy tube that is exactly round at x = +-2 m. Wires and trunks are LOD0
   only. Triangles: 428 / 204 / 60 (was 332 / 248 / 232).
6. **Crop_Veg LOD2** was 360 triangles per 4 m tile (36 % of LOD0) on a mass-tiled crop. It is now six low row
   mounds, 120 triangles.
7. **Floating props:** Barrel_Stack sat 7 mm and Hedge_A 5 mm above the ground. The barrels now go 13 mm below
   ground and the hedge root zone 0.1-0.15 m below.
8. **Lamp LODs** now use lower-subdivision flames and heads. The Totem LOD1/LOD2 wing boards are plain boxes.
9. `props_preview.py` gained the verifier scenes (`lods-*`, `bakes`).
