# Ship family (`SM_Ship_*`)

Eight ships and boats, one per step of the age line in `docs/concept/units/`: muscle, then wind, steam, diesel
and the space age. Each comes in 3 states with 4 LODs. Everything is generated by script from original procedural
geometry and textures (licence: original). Nothing was downloaded.

| Asset | Length × beam (m) | Ages it serves | Replaces in `DmTownsKit` | LOD0 tris (Intact) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ReedBoat` | 6.8 × 1.5 (2.8 including the paddles) | Stone, Bronze (river) | `Boat` V0 | 2,082 |
| `Canoe` (plank canoe with a small striped sail) | 13.2 × 1.35 | Bronze, Iron fishing boat | `Boat` V1 | 1,635 |
| `Galley` (war galley: 18 oars a side, ram, aphlaston) | 34.8 × 4.6 (15 including the oars) | late Bronze, Iron | `Galley` | 3,731 |
| `Cog` (castles fore and aft, one square sail) | 29.3 × 7.6 | late Iron, early Sail | `SailShip` V0 | 2,712 |
| `SailShip` (full-rigged, three masts, gun deck) | 57 × 11 | Sail, early Industry | `SailShip` V1 | 4,834 |
| `Steamship` (single funnel, white deckhouse) | 76.6 × 11 | Industry | `Steamer` | 3,110 |
| `ContainerShip` | 152.5 × 24 | Atomic | `CargoShip` V0 | 3,814 |
| `FastFreighter` (white wave-piercer with cargo pods and blue engine glow) | 117.7 × 16 | Space | `CargoShip` V1 | 3,207 |

**Meshes.** There are 24: `SM_Ship_<Asset>_A_<State>`, where the state is `Intact`, `Damaged` (heeled, burn
patches, torn sails, one mast down) or `Ruin` (the wreck: awash, heavily heeled, masts snapped, mostly burnt). All
three states share one footprint and one pivot. Triangle totals across the 24 meshes are LOD0 67,355,
LOD1 27,558, LOD2 9,584 and LOD3 5,845. Per-mesh numbers are in `manifest.json`.

## Regenerate

From the project root (the path contains a space, so quote it):

```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/ships/build_ships.py -- [--tex] [--only Galley,Cog] [--no-export]
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/ships/check_ships.py
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b art/ships/source/ships.blend --python art/ships/render_ships.py -- [--only SailShip] [--views close,mid,far] [--extra]
```

- The build is deterministic and takes about 5 s. The textures take 20 s and are made only when they are missing or
  when you pass `--tex`.
- `--extra` also renders the extra sheets:
  - `asset-ships-lineup-ages.png`
  - `asset-ships-cultures-{sail-ship,galley,container-ship}.png` (one hull in all six culture colours)
  - `asset-ships-states-<asset>.png` (Intact, Damaged, Ruin)
  - `asset-ships-night.png` (lit windows and the engine glow)

## Conventions used (docs/ASSETS.md)

- **Scale, axes and pivot.**
  - 1 Blender unit = 1 m. Z is up and the bow faces −Y.
  - The pivot is the centre of the hull at the **waterline (Z = 0 is the sea surface)**. Hulls continue below it;
    `draft_m` in the manifest gives the depth. A ship placed at sea level floats correctly.
- **FBX export settings.** Binary, smoothing groups (`FACE`) and tangents. `FBX_SCALE_UNITS`, so Unreal reads
  centimetres. Axes are Blender's defaults. `check_ships.py` re-imports the files and confirms metric sizes.
- **LODs.** Screen sizes are 1.0 / 0.35 / 0.12 / 0.04. Each LOD is built at its own resolution, not decimated:
  - LOD1 is about 40 % of LOD0. It has fewer stations and sides, half the oars and fewer shrouds, and each
    container stack is one box.
  - LOD2 is about 10–18 %. It drops crew, rigging and oars; the galley's oar banks become two cards, and each
    container bay is one block.
  - LOD3 is a simple hull with flat sails or blocks, 130–380 triangles.
- **Materials.** There are 3 slots: `Structure` (hull sheet), `Roof` (canvas sheet: sails, deckhouses and glazing)
  and `Detail` (detail sheet: containers, spars, rope, metal, crew, flags, gold, glow, oars and cargo).
  - **Base colour:** `lerp(D, D × CultureTint, M.G)`.
  - **Emissive:** `(M.B × D + M.R × NightFactor × warm) × 3`.
  - **The M channels:**
    - R: night windows (deckhouses, the stern gallery, the freighter's bridge)
    - G: culture tint mask
    - B: always-on glow (the freighter's engines)
  - **Normal maps** are OpenGL convention, so flip green on import. Import D as sRGB and N, ORM and M as linear.
  - **Culture instances:** `MI_Ship_<Slot>_C0..C5` set `CultureTint` to `DM.CULTURE_COLORS[k]`.
- **Trim-sheet UVs.** Each face's V stays inside one band of its sheet. U tiles, so set the textures to Wrap; the
  band table is in the manifest. ASSETS asks for UVs inside 0–1 only for baked maps, and these sheets are not baked.
- **Where the culture colour lives.** Following `docs/concept/units/index.md`:

  | Ship | Culture colour on |
  |---|---|
  | Reed boat | Woven gunwale binding, stern pennant |
  | Canoe | Top strake, striped sail, pennant |
  | Galley | Striped sail, oar-port strake, shields, pennant, stern flag |
  | Cog | Striped sail, castle parapets, banners, pennant |
  | Sailing ship | Gun-port stripe, masthead pennants, ensign. The sails stay cream `#efe6cf`, as the concept asks. |
  | Steamship | Funnel, sheer stripe, flags |
  | Container ship | Sheer stripe, funnel, about 5 % of the containers, ensign |
  | Fast freighter | Waterline stripe, tail fin, ensign |

## Known gaps

- **The units layer must exaggerate scale.** The meshes are real scale. At 1 km a galley is about 15 px and a reed
  boat about 4 px. `docs/concept/units` wants ships 38–58 px at mid zoom and sails at about 1.3× real size, which
  is the units layer's job. On deep water the dark wooden and black steel hulls have little contrast; the sails, the
  deckhouses and the containers carry the read. If more read is wanted, brighten the hull rim in the material.
- **Small boats are below the 3k ship budget on purpose.** The reed boat and canoe are 1.6–2k triangles, which is
  vehicle-sized; more would not show at 20–60 px.
- **No `_Construction` state.** That state is for buildings, and ships are not built on screen in the current sim.
  A slipway frame could be added cheaply if the towns code wants one.
- **Effects are not in the meshes.** That covers smoke from the steamship funnel or from burning ships, wakes, and
  spray. The ships are static meshes: oars, flags and sails do not animate. A cloth or WPO wobble in the `Roof`
  and `Detail` materials would sell motion.
- **Damage is per face.** Burn patches follow faces, so a large flat face (a deckhouse wall) burns all or nothing.
  The `Damaged` state therefore never chars faces larger than (0.12 L)².
- **One variant per ship (`_A`).** Culture variety comes from material instances only. There are no per-culture
  ornament mesh variants yet.
- **Not yet seen in Unreal.** The previews are Blender EEVEE renders under the ASSETS rig over a sea plane. Nothing
  has been imported into Unreal yet, so the LodGroup node and the unit scale still need a check on the first import.

## Independent verification (2026-11-19)

A second engineer regenerated everything from the scripts (`--tex` included: the 12 textures came back byte-identical,
and `manifest.json` is identical between two builds), re-ran `check_ships.py` (24/24 OK), audited every LOD of every
mesh with a separate script (LodGroup root at the origin with identity transform, 3 slots in the fixed order, no
degenerate faces, no n-gons, UV V inside 0-1, outward-facing share 99.1-100 % on all 96 LODs, crew height), and
re-rendered every preview through `tools/render_slot.py`. Fixes made, all in the scripts:

- **Decks** (`ship_geo.hull`): the deck was one lofted quad per station with V stretched across the whole beam, so
  on every tapering quad the planks zig-zagged (affine UV on a trapezoid) and were about 1.4 m wide. Decks are now cut
  into straight strips parallel to the centreline (2 m for wood, 1.6 m lifeboats, 1 m reed, 6 m steel, 4 m composite;
  double width at LOD1, one strip at LOD2+), planar-mapped, so planks run straight fore and aft at ~0.25 m. The inner
  bulwark and the end caps carry the same edge vertices, so there are no T-junctions (open-edge counts unchanged).
- **Reed boat lashing rings** now follow the curled ends; the outer rings used to float beside the upswept tips as
  black hooks.
- **Torn sails and flags** (`sail_keep`, `flag`): cells not connected to the yard (or to the hoist) are dropped, so
  no loose squares of cloth float in mid-air, and the torn grid is jittered so holes have ragged, not square, edges.
- **Crew figures** (`ship_geo.person`): body + head now equals the requested height (a 1.75 m sailor stood 1.67 m).

LOD0 grew by 3.5k triangles over the 24 meshes (deck strips); every ship is still inside the 3-15k budget except
the two small boats, as before. The handoff numbers in `docs/handoff/done-ue/asset-ships.built.txt` predate these
fixes; `manifest.json` is the reference.
