# art/wonders-a: wonders 0 to 3 (family `Wonder`)

These are the first four wonders from `docs/concept/wonders/index.md`. Everything is generated by script, with original procedural geometry and textures and no external inputs (licence: original). The binding rules are in `docs/ASSETS.md`.

| Concept | Mesh asset | What it is | Size (LOD0 bounds) |
|---|---|---|---|
| wonder-0-stone | `SM_Wonder_Hearth_*` | The Hearth of Many Fires: two earth and river-stone banks (r 74 m and 42 m, 9 m wide, 2 m high) with offset entrances, 228 fire pits every 3 m along the crests, and a 25 m timber cone with poles, lashing, hides and an antler crown | 158 x 158 m, 28.5 m to the antler tips |
| wonder-1-bronze | `SM_Wonder_GreatHive_*` | The Great Hive: a white-plastered beehive 45 m across with a 3-turn spiral ramp and parapet, 12 lamp-lit doorways, a 12 m polished bronze cap (55 m overall), a terrace and 44 ochre storage jars | 49 x 51 m, 55 m |
| wonder-2-iron | `SM_Wonder_SevenWatchers_*` | The Seven Who Keep Watch: a dry-stone platform (90 m across, 4 m high, rear stair) with seven faceless hooded granite figures (35 m) facing outward, each holding an iron fire bowl at 22 m | 91 x 97 m, 40 m |
| wonder-3-sail | `SM_Wonder_SeaGate_*` | The Sea Gate: two 70 m chamfered limestone towers with string courses, crenellations and windows, standing on rubble islets. A segmental arch spans 160 m between them (soffit 45 m, deck 42 to 57 m) with a voussoir ring and parapets, and a bronze-domed lantern pavilion sits at the crown | 217 x 38 m, 77 m to the lantern finial |

Each wonder has four states on the same footprint: `_Intact`, `_Construction`, `_Damaged` and `_Ruin`. That makes 16 meshes. Each FBX contains an FBX `LodGroup` with `_LOD0` to `_LOD3`. Screen sizes are 1.0, 0.35, 0.12 and 0.04. Per-mesh numbers are in `manifest.json`.

## Files
- `build_wonders_a.py` is the entry point. Next to it are `wa_lib.py` (geometry, UVs, cuts, LODs, FBX export and checks), `wa_tex.py` (texture synthesis, which uses `art/tools/dmtex.py`), `wa_mat.py` (Blender preview materials), `wa_render.py` (the preview rig) and one module per wonder (`wa_hearth.py`, `wa_hive.py`, `wa_seven.py` and `wa_seagate.py`).
- `textures/` holds the 2048² PNG sets:
  - `T_Wonder_<Set>_D` (sRGB), `_N` (OpenGL +Y, so flip green in UE or tick "flip green") and `_ORM` (R AO, G roughness, B metallic).
  - The sets are HearthBank, HearthCone, HivePlaster, HiveBronze, SevenPaving, SevenGranite, GateLimestone and GateBronze.
  - All four wonders share the detail atlas `DetailA`, which also has a `_M` mask.
- `export/` holds `SM_Wonder_<Asset>_<State>.fbx` and `..._lights.json`. The JSON lists the fires, lamps, windows and lantern that UE should spawn as lights or Niagara at night. Positions are in metres, in Blender axes.
- `source/` holds `wonders_a_<key>.blend` (every state and LOD with its materials) and `manifest_parts/`.
- `manifest.json` is the family manifest that the integration agent reads.
- The previews are `shots/asset-wonders-a-<concept>-{40m,300m,1km,night,states}.png`, plus `-lods.png` for Seven and the Sea Gate.

## Materials (at most 3 slots: 0 Structure, 1 Roof, 2 Detail)
| Wonder | Structure | Roof | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hearth | `M_Wonder_HearthBank`: a trim. U tiles every 16 m round the ring, and V runs once across the bank profile (stone revetment, grass shoulders, scorched crest) | `M_Wonder_HearthCone`: u 0 to 0.5 is bundled poles, u 0.5 to 1 is hide. Tiles in V every 4 m | `M_Wonder_DetailA` |
| Great Hive | `M_Wonder_HivePlaster`: lime plaster over mud brick, tiling every 8 m | `M_Wonder_HiveBronze`: polished hammered plates, tiling every 4 m, metallic | DetailA |
| Seven | `M_Wonder_SevenPaving`: flagstones tiling every 8 m. The revetment uses the same map stretched 2.2 x 0.55 so it reads as coursed stone | `M_Wonder_SevenGranite`: a unique cylindrical statue map (U = angle, front at u 0.75; V = height / 35 m) with fold cavities, lichen on the shoulders and a painted-black hood interior, so the figures can never show a face | DetailA |
| Sea Gate | `M_Wonder_GateLimestone`: running-bond ashlar, tiling every 8 m. The voussoir ring is UV'd radially | `M_Wonder_GateBronze`: bronze with verdigris, tiling every 4 m | DetailA |

`DetailA` is a 4 x 4 atlas laid out in `wa_lib.CELLS`: fire, lamp-lit doorway, lantern glass, iron, bronze, verdigris, bone, hide, timber, stone, terracotta, recess, charcoal, granite, limestone and plaster.
- `T_Wonder_DetailA_M` channels:
  - R is the night emissive mask.
  - G is the culture-colour mask (hides).
  - B is the flame mask.
- Vertex colour `Col` channels: R is the flicker phase and G is 1 on flame or glow geometry.
- Emissive is `D * M.R * lerp(1, EmissiveTint, 0.4) * Strength * NightFactor`. Fires also burn by day at about 1/8 of their night strength. The tints are #ff8c38 (Hearth), #ffad52 (Hive), #ff9a40 (Seven) and #ffb050 (Sea Gate).

Culture variation uses material instances `MI_Wonder_<Set>_<Style>` for the towns-code styles Temperate, Arid, Tropical and Cold. Each one sets a base-colour tint multiplier and a roughness offset; the values are in `manifest.json`. The per-culture colour goes in the Detail material's `CultureColor` parameter (masked by M.G). There are no mesh ornament variants (see gaps).

## Regenerate
Run from the project root (renders must go through the slot tool):
```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/wonders-a/build_wonders_a.py -- --tex all
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/wonders-a/build_wonders_a.py -- --assets hearth,hive,seven,gate --build
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/wonders-a/build_wonders_a.py -- --assets hearth --render all
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/wonders-a/build_wonders_a.py -- --manifest
```
- `--assets` takes any subset. Builds of different wonders can run in parallel, since each writes only its own files.
- `--render` takes `all` or a list from close, mid, far, night, states and lods.
- Times: textures take about 2 minutes, a build about 5 s per wonder, and a render set about 40 s.

## Checks (all LOD0 meshes)
- Pivot is at the footprint centre, Z = 0.
- Everything extends at least 0.3 m below ground. The Sea Gate's Z = 0 is mean water level, and its islets go down to -8.6 m.
- No inverted closed shells. No non-manifold edges within any one part; after welding coincident vertices (as an engine may), a few T-joins remain where parts touch: the Sea Gate parapet bases on the deck edge and the cut faces of the Hive's split sectors. They are coplanar and hidden, so nothing renders wrong.
- Open edges are deliberate. They come from one-sided cards and sheets: hides, lashing bands, door and window inlays, uncapped log and rib cylinders, and the bank sheets, whose open edges are buried at the foot.
- A 1.8 m figure is in every 40 m preview.
- Triangles at LOD0: Hearth 59k, Seven 63k, Hive 23k and Sea Gate 23k. The total over all 16 meshes is 473k.

## Known gaps
- **Hive and Sea Gate are under the 30k lower budget.** Their silhouettes are simple solids, and extra triangles would not show. Detail could be added if close-ups need it: door frames, ramp steps, tower machicolations.
- **Some things are left to UE.** There is no smoke, steam or flicker in the meshes; the integration should add Niagara at the `_lights.json` points. The Hearth's "rings of fire" read at orbit from 228 emissive flames, plus an emissive crest strip at LOD3.
- **Terrain must be prepared.** The Hearth (158 m) and the Seven platform need a site flattened to within ±0.3 m, and the Sea Gate needs a harbour mouth about 175 m wide with sea under the arch.
- **The previews have no aerial haze.** EEVEE's world volume rendered black headlessly, so it is disabled.
- **Some texture issues are still visible.**
  - The Hive plaster is still quite clean and white.
  - The Seven paving reads as a fine mosaic close up.
  - Flames are opaque teardrop meshes. They read as fire at 300 m and beyond, but are stylised at 40 m.
- **The Seven's hood is shallower than the concept's.** Folds and hood depth come from a radial loft, so the hood opening is a deep recess plus a painted shadow rather than a true undercut cowl.
- **No culture ornament mesh variants** (for example an alternative crown or finial). Culture variation is through material instances only.
- **The damage states are not exhaustive.** They are hand-placed: the Hive's damage is a collapsed sector, not a boolean. There is no `_Burnt` or `_Drowned` state.

## Independent verification (2026-11-19)
The family was rebuilt from scratch with the commands above. Textures, `_lights.json` files and the manifest were byte-identical to the builder's; the FBX files differed only in bytes that do not change the re-imported geometry (counts, bounds, UVs, slots and checks all matched). Every FBX was re-imported into an empty scene and checked: a LodGroup with `_LOD0` to `_LOD3`, scale 1, three slots in the order Structure, Roof, Detail, the `Col` attribute, one UV map, Detail UVs inside 0-1, no n-gons, no inverted closed shells, and triangle counts equal to the manifest. Fixes, all in the scripts:
1. The 1.8 m scale figure was outside the frame of the Hearth, Seven and Sea Gate 40 m views, and only its head showed at the Hive's lower edge. On the Sea Gate it also floated 3 m above the islet. Every figure now stands on a surface inside the frame: between the Hearth banks, on the Hive's jar terrace, on the Seven platform, and on the Sea Gate tower plinth. The Sea Gate's 40 m camera was lowered to include the tower foot.
2. The Seven's 40 m view cut off the hood, which is the one feature the concept insists on. It now frames the whole hooded figure.
3. `HearthBank`: the revetment was one size of tightly packed Voronoi cells and read as crazy paving. It is now loose river stones of two sizes set in earth.
4. `HivePlaster`: flaked patches repeated round the body like camouflage. They are now rare and small, with plaster lifts and broad grime. The Detail atlas plaster cell was toned down the same way.
5. Fire cell: the white-hot base made the flame meshes read as white candles. It is now yellow going to orange.
6. `wa_lib.triangulate` dissolves zero-area triangles: degenerate fan and ring faces at the top of the Hearth cone, and slivers left by the ruin cuts. This removed 48 triangles from Hearth Intact LOD0, and a few from other meshes.
7. The manifest's emissive formula now matches this README: lerp(1, tint, 0.4), and day fires at about 1/8 of night strength.
8. README counts were corrected: 228 fire pits and 12 doorways. The light files hold 229 and 13 entries, because each also has one beacon or glint socket.
