# Age1: the Bronze Age kit

This folder holds the Bronze Age settlement kit, built to `docs/ASSETS.md`. Everything is original. It is
generated by Blender Python scripts from procedural textures, with no downloaded inputs. The visual targets are
`docs/concept/settlements/age-1-bronze.png`, `docs/concept/landmarks/building-granary.png` and `building-temple.png`.

## What is here

There are 56 meshes. Each one is an FBX in `export/` that holds LOD0 to LOD2, plus LOD3 for anything over 10 m,
under an FBX `LodGroup`. LOD0 totals 89,034 triangles across the kit; LOD1 35,258; LOD2 9,629; LOD3 1,217 (after verification).

`manifest.json` lists every mesh with its footprint, height, triangles per LOD, slots, textures, sockets, licence
and script. It also holds the materials, material instances, cultures and a `sim_mapping` section for the
integration agent.

### Materials and textures

There are 7 texture sets in `textures/`, each 1024 x 1024 PNG with `_D` in sRGB and `_N` and `_ORM` linear. The
normal maps use the OpenGL convention, so tick "Flip Green Channel" when importing them into Unreal. The sets are:

- `Mudbrick`, 2 m tile
- `Render`, clay plaster, 3 m tile
- `Thatch`, 2 m tile; strands run down the slope
- `Earth`, rampart sod, 4 m tile
- `Soil`, field soil, 3 m tile
- `Crop`, 2 m tile
- `Trim`, a shared trim sheet of horizontal strips that tile along U. The strips are planks, logs, stone, reed,
  wicker, painted frieze, dark opening, grain, water and hide. The V ranges are in `age1_tex.STRIPS` and in the
  manifest.

`T_Age1_Trim_M.R` is the night-window mask, so only door and window openings glow.

Slots always follow the order Structure, Roof, Detail. A slot that a mesh does not use is left out, and the
manifest gives each mesh's slots.

- **Master materials:** `M_Age1_{Mudbrick,Render,Thatch,Earth,Soil,Crop,Trim}`. Each takes a `Tint` (linear
  multiply on D), a `RoughAdd`, and `Night` (Trim only).
- **Cultures** are material instances plus ornament meshes:
  - `Valley` (default): ochre mudbrick and golden-grey thatch.
  - `CliffTerrace`: rose-ochre plaster; prefers House C.
  - `LagoonWeaver`: grey clay, silvered timber and mossy thatch, plus `Orn_GableHorns`.
  - `RingBuilder`: pale render, dark blue-grey thatch, plus `Orn_Finial`.
- **Burnt** is a cheap state: put the `MI_Age1_*_Burnt` instances on the Damaged or Ruin meshes. Fields use
  `MI_Age1_Crop_{Ripe,Green,Stubble}`.
- **Vertex colour `Col`** (corner domain) carries ground-contact grime and large-scale roof and crop variation.
  Multiply it into the base colour; the Blender materials already do this.

### Conventions used

- Units are metres, Z is up, and the front (door, outside or water side) faces −Y.
- The pivot is at the footprint centre at ground level, and every building reaches down to Z = −0.3.
- Every state of an asset sits on the same footprint.
- On the landing and the boats, Z = 0 is the waterline. The landing has `mooring_*` and `shore_end` sockets. The reed
  boats point their bow toward -Y, like the Ship family. The landing's pivot is at the waterline under the jetty, about
  0.9 m off the footprint centre in Y, so its sockets stay valid.
- Palisade and rampart segments tile along X. Their bank noise fades to zero at the segment ends so the runs join
  cleanly, and the planner can scale them in X to close a ring.

## How to regenerate

From the project root:

```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/age1/build_age1.py -- --textures
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b art/age1/source/age1.blend --python art/age1/preview_age1.py -- houses,granary,temple,walls,fields,landing,village,house-states,granary-states,temple-states,wall-states,landing-states,cultures,lods
```

- `--textures` rebuilds the PNGs, which takes about 25 s; they are also rebuilt if they are missing.
- `--no-fbx` skips the export.
- `--only House,Granary` writes a partial `source/age1_partial.blend` and skips the manifest.
- The full build takes about 25 s plus the texture time.

The scripts are:

- `age1_tex.py`: the texture sets. It uses the shared `art/tools/dmtex.py`.
- `age1_geo.py`: the mesh builder. It covers prisms, lofts and lathes, tubes, occupancy walls with openings and
  broken tops, masked thick slabs for roofs, welding, outward normals, the grime attribute, LOD hulls and
  decimation.
- `age1_assets.py`: every generator.
- `build_age1.py`: materials, the registry, LODs, FBX export and the manifest.
- `preview_age1.py`: the preview rig. It uses a 20° golden-hour sun from the camera's left, a cool sky fill,
  EEVEE and 1280 x 720 output.

The previews are in `shots/asset-age1-<set>-{40m,300m,1km}.png`; the state and culture sheets are `-near.png`.
`asset-age1-village-*` puts the kit together the way `age-1-bronze.png` does: a rampart ring with a gate, about 40
houses, the temple at the centre, the granary, fields outside and the landing. The 40 m view of a large set uses a
distance of max(40 m, 1.45 x the set size) so that the whole set is in frame.

## Independent verification (2026-11-19)

A second engineer rebuilt the whole kit from the scripts. The textures and the manifest came out byte-identical, so the
build is reproducible. They then re-imported every FBX and checked it against `docs/ASSETS.md`, rendered new previews
through the slot tool and fixed these problems in the scripts:

1. **House D roof.** The roof (and the construction roof frame and damaged rafters) sat 2.2 m forward of the walls,
   over the yard, and left the back of the house open. `thatch_gable` and `roof_frame` now take the body offset
   `cy`. This also fixes the D ridge sockets, which were in the right place while the roof was not.
2. **Granary Damaged.** The slipped thatch cap was placed 5.8 m below ground, so it could not be seen and it pushed the
   bounds down. It now sits crooked on its drum.
3. **Inside-out open parts.** `recalc_face_normals` had turned some open parts inside out: the half-built and broken
   granary drums, and the end cones of both reed boats. These would have looked hollow in Unreal with backface
   culling. Open parts now keep the orientation they were built with.
4. **Reed boats.** They were built bow toward +X. They now point toward -Y, as `docs/ASSETS.md` and the Ship family do.
5. **Temple court pool.** The pool cut 5.5 x 2.3 m into the temple's base tier. It moved forward to
   (15.5, -17), and the `tree_1` socket moved clear of the platform to (-17, -16).
6. **Temple Ruin LOD2.** LOD2 used crisp, intact-looking tiers with a Roof slot that LOD0 lacks. It now uses the same
   eroded mound as LOD0 and LOD1.
7. **Manifest triangle counts.** 16 meshes listed more LOD triangles than their FBX held, because duplicate and
   zero-area faces were counted and then dropped on import. The build now validates each mesh and cleans the
   decimated LODs, so the manifest matches the files exactly.
8. **Previews.** The walls preview put the rampart End against the gate's own flank, facing it, which read as a broken
   wall; it now ends a straight run. The village preview has a trodden-earth floor inside the ring, as in the
   concept. A new `lods` sheet (`shots/asset-age1-lods-near.png`) shows LOD0 to LOD3 side by side.

## Known gaps

- **Civic meshes are under the 8k to 20k LOD0 range.** The granary has 7.1k, the temple 3.4k and the court 1.0k.
  The shapes are simple masses, and detail would go into texture before triangles, so I did not pad them to reach
  the range.
- **Textures are procedural and fairly clean.** At 40 m the walls and thatch read as a well-made game asset, not
  as photo scans. The concept's warmth depends a lot on the Unreal lighting and fog, and the preview rig has no
  atmospheric haze: a world volume blacked out the EEVEE sky, so I left it out.
- **Field soil margins** and the ploughed furrows are subtle from far away. The fields have no hedges; the Iron Age
  kit should add those.
- **The Burnt and Drowned states** exist only as material instances on the Damaged and Ruin meshes. There is no
  dedicated geometry for them.
- **Culture variation is mostly tint plus two ornaments.** RingBuilder in particular wants its own round house; that
  is in the Iron Age concept and was out of scope here.
- **Trees, people, cattle pens and smoke** are not part of this family. The village preview has no trees; the
  temple court exposes `tree_*` sockets for them.
- **Nothing has been imported into Unreal yet.** I re-imported one FBX into Blender and confirmed the LodGroup, the
  3 slots, the UVs and the `Col` attribute.
