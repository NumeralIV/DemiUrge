# Age4: the Industry age kit

These are the buildings of the Industry age, generated by scripts in Blender 5.2 to `docs/ASSETS.md`.
The visual targets are `docs/concept/settlements/age-4-industry.png`,
`docs/concept/landmarks/building-factory.png` and `building-mine.png`. The mine concept shows the Iron
age open pit, so the Industry-age mine here is a deep-shaft colliery with a headframe, which fits the
same `b.mine` slot one age later. Licence: everything is original. No inputs were downloaded and
every texture is procedural.

The exact triangle counts for every state and LOD, footprints, sockets, materials and the sim
mapping are in `manifest.json`, which the integration agent should read. That includes
`towns_kit_id` (the `UDmTownsFactory` id each mesh replaces: Rowhouse, Factory, Stack, Warehouse,
MineHead, Spoil).

**States.** Every building and bridge has `_Intact`, `_Construction`, `_Damaged` and `_Ruin` on the
same footprint. Heaps and ornaments have `_Intact` only.
- Construction: the walls are cut to a stepped course height, the roofs are not built yet, and the
  building has timber scaffolding with decks and material stacks. The window openings are empty.
- Damaged: holes are blown through the roofs and upper walls with exact booleans. The masses are
  hollow, so a hole looks into a sooty room. One stack top is broken on the factories, the station
  clock tower is hit, there is soot round the holes, rubble at the base, and the windows near the
  holes are empty.
- Ruin: the walls are cut to jagged heights as hollow shells with charred floors. All roofs are gone,
  rubble mounds sit inside and outside, and fallen beams lie around. The bridge ruin is the truss
  broken off one pier and tilted into the river. The viaduct ruin has its middle arch collapsed.
- On the bridges, Construction shows timber falsework under a half-erected truss, or arch centring
  under the viaduct.

**Sockets** (FBX empties `SOCKET_*` under LOD0):
- `Smoke_*` on every stack and every chimney pot, for the smoke FX.
- `Smoke_Winding` on the engine house, for steam.
- `Ornament_Chimney_*`, `Ornament_Gable_*` and `Ornament_Spire_0` for the culture ornaments.

## Conventions used (all from docs/ASSETS.md)

- **Units and axes.** Metres, Z up, front (street side, doors) toward −Y, pivot at the footprint
  centre on the ground. Foundations reach 0.3 m below Z = 0.
- **Bridge pivot.** Bridges use the deck centre at rail level as the pivot, and their piers reach
  16 m down. Tracks on the station and the goods shed have rail top at Z = 0.
- **Material slots** (max 3): `Structure`, `Roof`, `Detail`.

| Mesh | Structure | Roof | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buildings | `M_Age4_Brick` | `M_Age4_Slate` | `M_Age4_Trim` |
| Truss bridge | `M_Age4_Stone` | `M_Age4_Gravel` (deck) | `M_Age4_Trim` |
| Viaduct | `M_Age4_Brick` | `M_Age4_Gravel` | `M_Age4_Trim` |
| Heaps | `M_Age4_Gravel` | `M_Age4_Gravel` (unused) | `M_Age4_Trim` |

- **Textures** (`textures/`, 1024², PNG; D is sRGB, everything else linear):

| Set | Files | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Brick | `T_Age4_Brick_{D,N,ORM}` | Running-bond red brick with soot streaks; one tile = 1.8 m |
| BrickBuff | `T_Age4_BrickBuff_D` | Buff brick for culture C; shares the Brick N and ORM |
| Slate | `T_Age4_Slate_{D,N,ORM}` | Slate courses with lichen; one tile = 2.0 m |
| Stone | `T_Age4_Stone_{D,N,ORM}` | Sandstone ashlar; one tile = 2.4 m |
| Gravel | `T_Age4_Gravel_{D,N,ORM}` | Dark shale, neutral so instances can tint it; one tile = 4.0 m |
| Trim | `T_Age4_Trim_{D,N,ORM,M}` | The family trim sheet. The lower half has 8 bands that tile in U: iron, timber, stone, rust, roof glazing, track bed with sleepers, lead, terracotta. The upper half has unique tiles: 6-pane and 2-pane sashes, factory window, two doors with fanlights, loading door, shop front, boarded window, large multi-pane window, rubble, the station lunette, clock face, louvre, coal and char. `M`: R is the night-window emissive mask, G is a per-pane random value. |

  Tiling sets use world-scale planar UVs: each face is projected in its own plane, with slate
  courses running horizontally and up the slope. The trim sheet uses UV atlas coordinates.
- **Vertex colour `Col`** (exported linear, per corner):

| Channel | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R | Per-window random id. A window is lit when R < the instance's lit fraction, which matches towns custom data 6. |
| G | Soot or char amount, for the damage states. |
| B | Culture accent mask (painted doors). |

- **Master material contract** for UE, as implemented in the Blender preview material in
  `lib/a4mat.py`:
  - BaseColor = lerp(D, luminance(D), Desaturate) × Tint.
  - Where Col.B > 0.5, BaseColor is replaced by luminance(D) × 2 × AccentTint.
  - BaseColor is then multiplied by (1 − 0.8 × Col.G).
  - Roughness = ORM.G + RoughnessAdd + 0.3 × Col.G.
  - Emissive = M.R × WindowColour × step(Col.R, LitFraction) × Night (Detail slot only).
  - Add a world-space macro noise of ±8 % with a period of about 25 m to hide brick tiling.
- **Culture variation** comes from material instances (`MI_Age4_<Set>_<Base|A|B|C>`; the parameters
  are in the manifest; `culture_variants.Base` names the `_Base` instances, not the bare masters) plus the ornament meshes on the sockets:

| Culture | Walls | Roofs | Doors (accent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Red brick | Dark slate | — |
| A (Cliff-Terrace) | Rose-ochre brick | Warm clay-red | Cobalt |
| B (Lagoon-Weaver) | Silver-grey brick | Mossy silver | Saffron |
| C (Ring-Builder) | Buff brick | Blue-grey slate | Slate-blue |

  `MI_Age4_Gravel_Coal` and `MI_Age4_Gravel_Ore` turn the heaps into coal and ore heaps.
- **LODs.** LOD0 has real window reveals (glass recessed 16 cm), stone sills and lintels, string
  courses, pilasters, chimney pots, yards and privies. LOD1 keeps the reveal-free facade grid (on the
  street side only for terraces) and uses window quads elsewhere. LOD2 is a plain mass with window
  quads. LOD3 is the hull (massing and roofs only). Screen sizes are 1.0 / 0.35 / 0.12 / 0.04.
- **FBX.** One file per mesh and state in `export/`:
  - binary, with an LOD group (an empty with `fbx_type = LodGroup` and LOD0..LOD3 children);
  - smoothing groups from 35° sharp edges, tangents, and linear vertex colours;
  - the `SOCKET_` empties under LOD0;
  - Blender's default axis conversion, so UE gets +X forward.

## How to regenerate

From the project root (the path has a space, so keep the quotes):

```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/age4/build_age4.py -- [--textures] [--only Terrace_A,Factory_A] [--states Intact,Ruin]
python tools/render_slot.py -- "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python art/age4/preview_age4.py -- [--only ...] [--views close,40m,300m,1km,states,cultures,lods,st-Ruin] [--district]
```

The full build takes about 40 s on the CPU, so it is not a render. It writes:
- `blend/<Asset>.blend`: every state and LOD, the materials and the sockets;
- `export/*.fbx`;
- `manifest.json`, merged, so `--only` runs keep the other entries.

The textures are rebuilt when they are missing or when you pass `--textures`. Seeds are fixed, so
reruns are deterministic.

Previews go to `shots/asset-age4-<asset>-<view>.png`:

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `close` | A true 40 m view of the front-left of the street facade, with the 1.8 m figure at the door line (on the deck for bridges) |
| `40m` | Three-quarter view fitted to the asset's box (fills about 86 % of the frame), with a 1.8 m figure for scale |
| `300m`, `1km` | The asset seen from 300 m and 1 km |
| `states` | All states side by side |
| `cultures` | Base, A, B and C side by side |
| `lods` | The four LODs side by side |
| `st-<State>` | A close view of one state |

`--district` lays the whole kit out as a small industrial town and renders it:
- `asset-age4-district-300m.png` and `asset-age4-district-1km.png`: terrace grid, mills, quay,
  station, bridge over a river, and the colliery;
- `asset-age4-district-mills.png`: a closer view of the mills.

Compare these against the age-4 concept.

**Code** (`lib/`):

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `a4tex.py` | Texture synthesis, on top of `art/tools/dmtex.py` |
| `a4geo.py` | Parts and primitives, facades with real openings, roofs, damage states (exact booleans per part, hollowing, heightfield cuts, blob holes, scaffolding, rubble), mesh assembly and checks |
| `a4_terrace.py` | The terraces |
| `a4_industry.py` | Everything else |
| `a4assets.py` | The registry: slots, budgets, states, sim mapping |
| `a4mat.py` | Materials and culture instances |
| `a4district.py` | The district preview |

## Known gaps

- **Triangle counts against budget.** `Terrace_C` is about 6.2k at LOD0, 4 % over the 6k house
  budget: it is a row of six three-storey houses, about 1k per dwelling. Several civic meshes (`Factory_B`, `MineHead_A`,
  the bridges, `Warehouse_B`) are under the 8k civic minimum, because more detail would not show at
  game distance.
- **Truss bridge LODs.** The truss LODs cannot reach 12 % at LOD2 without losing the lacing that is
  its silhouette, so its LOD2 is about 60 % of LOD0.
- **No real interiors.** Damaged and ruined buildings are hollow shells with a charred floor. The
  window glass stays in the wall (it is remapped to dark empty openings), so you cannot see through
  the window openings.
- **Non-manifold edges.** The checks report a few on LOD0. They are deliberate single-sided decals
  (clock faces, canopy and rooflight glass) and touching closed parts (sawtooth teeth, adjacent wall
  blocks). Closed masses have no holes.
- **No collision or lightmap UVs.** There are no `UCX_` collision hulls and no lightmap UV channel.
  Let UE generate them; with Lumen, no lightmaps are needed.
- **FX and terrain left to UE.** Smoke, steam and the furnace glow are FX on the sockets. Haze and
  the terrain (river, yards, streets) are the towns and ground modules' job, so the previews show the
  assets on a neutral ground.
- **Ornament sockets.** The culture ornaments are only placed through sockets: no mesh variant has
  them baked in. Culture A, B and C rooflines differ only when the integration agent attaches them.
- **Bridge piers.** The bridges assume the deck sits 16 m above the river bed. Deeper or shallower
  crossings need a different pier length; there is no parameter for that yet.

## Independent verification (2026-11-19)

An independent verifier rebuilt the family from the scripts, re-imported all 57 FBX files into an empty
Blender scene, and re-rendered every preview through `tools/render_slot.py`.

**Reproducibility.** A first `--textures` rebuild reproduced the builder's 17 textures and `manifest.json`
byte for byte. The FBX files differed only in the FBX header timestamp and file id. After the fixes, two
more full builds gave identical manifests.

**Audit** (the verifier's own script, over the re-imported FBX):
- Hierarchy: a `LodGroup` empty with an identity transform, and `_LOD0` to `_LOD3` parented to it.
- Every LOD is triangles only, and every triangle count matches the manifest. Before fix 1, four files
  were off by one.
- Every mesh has 3 slots in Structure / Roof / Detail order. The heaps show 2 slots in UE, because their
  Structure and Roof slots use the same material.
- Every mesh has the `Col` vertex colours. The trim-sheet faces have V inside 0-1 on every LOD.
- Pivot and ground: footprints are centred, with the ground at Z = 0 and the plinths reaching -0.3 to -0.45.
  `Spoil_A` (+3.7 m in X, because of the tipping ramp) and `Warehouse_A` (-1.15 m in Y, because of the hoist
  housing) are off-centre. The manifest records their offset in `footprint_centre_offset_m`.
- Normals: the signed volume of every closed part is positive on every LOD. The only exception is one
  small part on `Factory_B_Damaged` LOD1. The ray-test 'inward' hits are faces buried inside other parts
  (truss members passing through each other, the stack cap under the crown), not flipped faces.
- Scale against the 1.8 m figure: storeys are 2.95-3.2 m, doors 1.0 x 2.4 m, windows 1.05 x 1.4-1.6 m and
  the track gauge is 1.435 m.

**Fixes** (all made in the scripts):
1. `a4geo.build_object` now drops sliver triangles (< 1e-6 m²) and duplicate triangles. Duplicates had
   been merged on FBX import, so the manifest disagreed with the export by one triangle on
   `Factory_A_Ruin`, `Factory_B_Ruin`, `Station_A_Ruin` and `Warehouse_A_Damaged`.
2. **Socket names.** Every `_Damaged` FBX exported its sockets as `SOCKET_Smoke_0.001`,
   `SOCKET_Ornament_Chimney_0.001` and so on. The cause was that the Intact state's empties still held the
   names. UE would not have found them under the names in the manifest. The names are now freed after each
   state is exported.
3. **Damaged stacks.** The smoke sockets above broken stack tops (`Stack_A`, the `MineHead_A` stack)
   floated 1.3-3 m in the air. They are now dropped to 0.5 m above the break.
4. **Terrace damage.** The generic hole placement drew from the whole plot box, yards included, at
   chimney-top height, so most holes missed the house. `Terrace_A_Damaged` showed a single hole, on the
   rear. The terraces now place their holes on the roof slopes, plus one in the upper street facade.
5. **Booleans.** `a4geo.boolean_part` now rejects any result that reaches outside the input part, which a
   difference never can. It then retries with the float solver, or leaves the part uncut. On
   `Terrace_D_Damaged`, the exact solver had kept a brick lump of the cutter standing above the parapet.
6. **Ruined colliery.** On `MineHead_A_Ruin`, the headframe was cut away to stumps, so the ruin lost the
   one silhouette that says "colliery". The lattice now lies toppled along the tub track, with a sheave
   wheel thrown clear (`a4_industry._minehead_ruin`).
7. **Yard heap.** `Spoil_B` was a dome that read as a boulder. It is now a tipped cone at the angle of
   repose.
8. **Material.** The Base brick instance is toned to the concepts' soot-darkened red-brown (Tint
   0.84/0.78/0.76, Desaturate 0.12). Clean orange-red was brighter than the age-4 and factory concepts.
   `culture_variants.Base` in the manifest now names the `MI_*_Base` instances, so UE keeps that tint.
9. **Previews.** The `40m` and line-up views now fit the asset's box to the frame; before, the assets
   filled about a third of the width. A new `close` view puts the camera 40 m from the facade.

**Still open:**
- Budgets:
  - `Station_A` (7.0k) is under the 8k civic minimum too, which the original notes did not list.
  - Damaged and construction states add 1-1.7k on the terraces, so `Terrace_C` reaches 6.9-7.9k and
    `Terrace_D_Damaged` 6.9k, against the 6k house budget.
- Sawtooth glazing: at 40 m the long, narrow panes of the glazed faces read as brownish vertical
  streaks rather than glass. They are fine at 300 m.
- Intact buildings have no soot or weathering beyond the crown of each stack.
- The `Bridge_B` Construction state reads as two solid brick boxes over the centring.
