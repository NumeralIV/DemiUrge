   tale: The Book of Embervale
     In the beginning Embervale was fire and then stone, and the god called ice down out of the dark. Where it fell, the hollows filled, and that was the first sea, and in time it covered the greater part of the world. Then green came out of the wet places, slowly at first, as if it were not sure it was allowed.
     The first people were the Roossess. The god woke them in the shallows at Ta Kripa, and they called the god Girtdemton. After them came the Gigasks, the Sosligirs, the Slopaspils, the Zames, the Naonds and the Dunpisks, peoples with their own tongues and their own names for the god.
     In 3,496 years the peoples of Embervale founded 132 settlements, and at their height they numbered 7,386,825 souls. The greatest of their places was Sonatombek. The furthest of them reached the Age of the Stars.
     They were not always at peace. They fought 69 wars, in which 132 towns changed hands and one was burned to the ground. The last time they burned cities with the fire of the sun was in the year 3,281.
     The god was known for lightning and earthquakes. The scribes wrote more of the god's anger than of the god's mercy, and they wrote it carefully. The peoples prayed, and 36 prayers went unheard. In the end, most of them called the god the Lightning-Handed.
     There were hard times. Eleven mountains woke in fire, hunger came 151 times, sickness came 27 times and 34 towns were taken by the sea. Of all the places the peoples built, 36 lay in ruins.
     These things the scribes remembered above all. In the first year, at Ta Kripa, the Roossess woke in the rain. They were few, and they were hungry, and they were alive. In the year 1,639, the Roossess raised sails and went out upon the great sea. No people had done so before them. In the year 2,589, Ta Pago was swallowed by the sea. In the year 3,281, a light brighter than noon rose over Mutlend-Lapa, sent by the Numis against the Sanandosks, and the world learned a new fear.
     In the 3496th year the Roossess fled Embervale. They built the ship in secret, as far as anything can be secret from a god, and they launched it at night. They did not pray as they rose. On the new world, the mothers will tell the children of the Lightning-Handed, who lived in the sky of the old one, and the children will sleep with the lamp lit, and grow up, and build their houses low.
