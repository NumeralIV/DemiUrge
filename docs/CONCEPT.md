# DEMIURGE — concept brief

*A world in your hands.*

## Pitch

DEMIURGE is a 3D god game played on one small, living planet that fits on the screen. You are a
newborn god. You are handed a ball of molten rock. You cool it, call comets down to fill its seas,
seed life and watch green spread across the continents, then awaken a people. They forage, farm,
found villages, split into cultures with their own languages, trade, pray, go to war, and climb
from the Stone Age to the Space Age. You never control them. You shape the world they live in:
raise mountains, carve seas, send rain, bless fields, or throw lightning, meteors, volcanoes and
plagues. They watch what you do, and they write it down. Their chronicle names you by your deeds
("the Rain-Giver", "the Drowner", "She Who Burns the Sky"), and how they feel about you decides
how the story ends when their ships finally leave for the stars — or never do.

## Pillars

1. **A planet you want to stare at.** Low-poly diorama look with real lighting: sun shadows racing
   across mountains at the terminator, an atmosphere rim, drifting clouds and their shadows, oceans
   with depth and foam, ice caps, rivers, forests of tiny trees, cities that light up on the night
   side. Seamless zoom from orbit down to individual houses with a tilt-shift miniature feel.
2. **Everything is simulated and connected.** Terrain → temperature and rain → rivers and biomes →
   vegetation and wildlife → where people can live → how their civilization grows → what their
   industry does to the climate → sea level → their coastal cities. Every god power is a push on
   that web, and consequences ripple.
3. **Emergent history, told back to you.** Procedural languages and names, a written chronicle of
   foundings, famines, wars, prophets and miracles, and a god-epithet that changes with your
   behaviour. At the end, a time-lapse replays the whole history of your world.
4. **Spectacle on demand.** Meteor strikes that leave craters and send tsunamis, volcanoes that
   build islands, lightning that starts forest fires, storms, floods, auroras.
5. **Zero friction.** Double-click `index.html`. No install, no build, no assets, no network.
   Mouse, keyboard and touch. Everything (graphics, sound, music, text) is generated by code.

## Flow of a game

- **Genesis (player-paced, ~3–5 minutes, also the tutorial)**
  1. *Molten*: a glowing world. Name it / pick a seed. It cools.
  2. *Water*: click to call comets; each one raises the sea. Sculpt land freely (raise / lower)
     while "the clay is soft". You decide how much ocean your world has.
  3. *Life*: touch the land; vegetation spreads outward in a visible wave; biomes colour in;
     animals appear.
  4. *People*: choose where the first tribe wakes.
- **History (~30–40 minutes at 1×, with pause / 1× / 2× / 4× / 8×)**
  Ages: Stone → Bronze → Iron → Sail → Industry → Atomic → Space. Settlements grow from camps to
  metropolises; cultures diverge, trade and fight; faith in you earns **Awe**, the currency of
  miracles. Late ages bring factories, CO₂, melting ice, rising seas, and nuclear tension.
- **Endings** depend on who survives and how they regard you: the beloved god whose name is
  carried to the stars, the feared god they flee from, the forgotten god, or silence. Then the
  time-lapse and a shareable "planet card".

## Powers (cost Awe; unlocked as the world advances)

Shape: Raise land, Lower land. Sky: Rain, Bloom (grow vegetation / fertility).
Wrath: Lightning, Meteor, Volcano, Earthquake, Plague.
Grace: Heal / Bless, Inspire, Prophet, Omen of Peace.
Genesis only: Comet (water), Seed life, Awaken people.

## Technical frame

- Plain JavaScript in classic `<script>` files under one global namespace `DM`. No libraries.
- Planet = icosphere, subdivision level 6 → 40,962 cells (each cell is a mesh vertex with 5–6
  neighbours). All simulation state is per-cell typed arrays plus small entity lists.
- The simulation is pure, deterministic JS with no DOM access, so it runs headless in Node for
  automated tests and thousand-year balance runs.
- Custom WebGL2 forward renderer: instanced low-poly objects, sun shadow map, MSAA HDR, bloom,
  tilt-shift, tonemapping. DOM/CSS HUD. WebAudio synthesized music and effects.
- Planet datum radius is 100 world units; elevation roughly −6 … +9 units; +Y is the north pole.

## Tone

Mythic, warm, a little wry. The chronicle reads like a translated ancient text. The UI is quiet
gold-on-ink with serif type. The planet is the star.
