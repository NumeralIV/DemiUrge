# Close-up asset sourcing and licences

T-003 · Astra · researched **2026-11-19** · base: `5ac2baf`.

## Recommendation

Use **CC0 materials and everyday props, a small number of licensed vegetation/ship packs, and original Blender building kits**. Buy surfaces and specialist modelling effort; keep DemiUrge's architecture, culture variation, construction states and landmark silhouettes original. Do not replace the current stand-ins until the lead schedules the asset pass.

Start with a free conifer/broadleaf/prop evaluation. If it passes, a medieval kit, a sailing vessel with rowboat, and an industrial kit cost **$174.97 Personal / $354.97 Professional at the observed regular prices**, before tax. These are three specific products, not the cost of finishing the game's art. A broader asset allowance including reserves is **about $810 / $1,026**, excluding labour and characters; the calculation is below. No purchase is recommended solely because a sale is running.

This is sourcing research, not an import or performance certification. No asset archives, textures, meshes, screenshots from vendors, or other downloaded content were committed. No Unreal code, specs, task-board entries or lab files were changed. Product descriptions, published compatibility and licence terms were inspected; no candidate was imported, cooked or rendered in UE 5.7.

### Project constraints used

- [CONCEPT](../CONCEPT.md): Stone → Bronze → Iron → Sail → Industry → Atomic → Space, with culture and settlement-tier variation. A medieval village is only part of that coverage.
- [SPEC-UNREAL §0.4 and C4/C5](../SPEC-UNREAL.md): close-up stand-ins are temporary; preserve the mesh factory, placement, HISM, LOD/culling and material controls. Asset research does not lift the current implementation rule against downloading assets.
- [LifeLab vegetation and towns](../lab/vegetation-and-towns.md): on the reported RTX 3060, ordinary instanced trees were much faster than the tested Nanite alternatives; rainforest reached roughly 30 ms GPU. Keep a conventional foliage LOD path. These are **the lab author's measurements**, not new tests by Astra.
- [Approved orbit reference](../../shots/ue-cloudlab-terminator-final.png): restrained, natural surfaces and atmospheric scale. Near-camera assets must hold up in warm sun, storm light and night, rather than rely on the vendor's showcase lighting.

## 3. Make it ourselves: Blender pipeline

The lead's Blender connector can execute repeatable modelling/export operations. It cannot replace aesthetic judgement or establish the rights of an imported reference. Use it for reusable modules and deterministic variants, with a person/lead reviewing silhouettes and rendered results before multiplying them.

1. **Prove one close-up kit.** Make a house, shrine, tree and boat in a single coherent palette. Review in the game's golden-hour, storm and night views, then at village/town camera heights. Agree real scale, roof pitch, overhang thickness, foundation treatment and material roughness before expanding the set.
2. **Define an export contract with the lead.** Model in metres, apply transforms and validate the conversion to UE centimetres with a known 2 m door. Use ground-level pivots, footprint bounds, consistent forward orientation, grid-compatible wall/roof sockets and deterministic mesh IDs. Preserve the existing mesh factory and curved-planet placement; imported assets should not dictate settlement layout.
3. **Author meaningful geometry.** Bevel edges and model roof thickness, lintels, plinths and silhouette details. Build modular wall/roof/frontage families with original culture motifs. Use Geometry Nodes/Python for measured variation, not random deformation of every surface. Keep `.blend` sources editable; freeze modifiers for exports where necessary.
4. **Use a compact material vocabulary.** Shared wood/plaster/stone/roof trim sheets, neutral base colour, roughness and normal maps; explicit culture tint, wetness, snow/ash and night-emissive masks. Bake procedural Blender shading to textures; rebuild UE materials rather than expecting node graphs to transfer. Preserve normals and check DirectX/OpenGL normal-map conventions and packed-channel meanings.
5. **Author change states.** Planned foundation, scaffold/partial build, complete, damaged and ruin forms should share footprint and key attachment points. Separate window emissive masks, chimney/smoke sockets, door placement and culture accents. This is a major advantage over generic finished-building packs.
6. **Prepare distance representations.** Conventional LODs/impostors for foliage, simplified repeated buildings and shared material slots. Keep crown colour/coverage consistent with the far ground canopy. Evaluate Nanite only where it improves the actual 3060 scene; it does not eliminate material, shadow or transparency costs.
7. **Import through the lead's repeatable content scripts.** FBX or another agreed supported interchange format, baked images and explicit settings. Check scale, pivots, collision, UVs, normals, bounds, material slots, texture memory and package cooking. Export metadata can map mesh ID, culture, age, tier, variant, dimensions and sockets to the factory. This document proposes the contract; it adds no new implementation schema.
8. **Review before bulk generation.** Use the same views, lighting, density, resolution and warm-up as the lab. Include a forest edge, rainforest, harbour, slope town and night scene. Record GPU time and memory alongside images. Only then extend biome/era coverage.

These are **planning estimates for an experienced artist/technical artist**, not measured connector throughput or a delivery promise. One working day means roughly eight hours; feedback and rework can dominate.

At an **illustrative**, not researched market quote, $400/day, this slice is $8,400–$16,000 in labour value. If the owner/lead does it, cash payment may be zero but time is still consumed. Buying the three listed packs may save modelling days; it does not remove import, material harmonisation, layout, state-variant or performance work. Full seven-age/culture coverage, crowds and wildlife cannot responsibly be priced from this research alone.

Make the things that define the game: temples, wonders, culture-specific roof/frontage families, evolving settlements and construction/ruin states. Buy when labour is expensive and identity is low: natural scans, specialist foliage, ship rigging and generic industrial details. Keep purchased source geometry out of otherwise redistributable original kits unless its licence permits that distribution.

## 4. Licence pitfalls, in plain language

All links in this section were checked **2026-11-19**. These are acquisition/release checks for the lead, not a claim that any unidentified asset has been cleared.

- **The store is not the licence.** A Fab entry may use Standard, CC BY or legacy terms. Save the listing, publisher, asset/version ID, acquisition date, selected tier, receipt and actual licence text together. A free price is not a public-domain dedication. [Fab licence documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fab/licenses-and-pricing-in-fab), [Fab EULA §§1–2][fab].
- **Personal is not “non-commercial.”** Standard Personal can cover commercial projects if eligible. Eligibility considers the buyer and controlling/common-control entities, prior 12-month digital-content commercial revenue, advances and funds raised; the threshold is $100,000. Do not infer the owner's eligibility from this repository. Reference Only does not provide the source meshes needed here. [Fab EULA §2][fab].
- **Shipping a game does not mean shipping an asset library.** Standard allows incorporated game content but restricts extraction/use outside the project and standalone redistribution. Private project-collaborator access has conditions. An exportable world editor or public source archive needs separate attention; world *save data* should reference asset IDs, not export licensed meshes. [Fab EULA §§4–6][fab].
- **“NoAI” is a specific restriction, not a blanket ban on a game whose code used an assistant.** The EULA restricts NoAI content in generative-AI datasets, development and training inputs; it also defines exclusions for tools that only operate on original content or arrange existing content without making new content. This is not blanket permission to upload vendor meshes/textures to an external generation service. Record each asset's flag and check the intended operation. Hivemind's village and Gen02_04 currently say AI usage No. [Fab EULA §§6(b)(vii), 16(l)][fab].
- **Old Quixel downloads do not automatically become new Fab entitlements.** The legacy agreement's Megascans Addendum distinguishes Unreal-only, personal/indie, educational and evaluation access; some forbid commercial distribution or source sharing. Obtain evidence of the actual acquisition route before reusing an old cache. [Epic Content License Agreement, Megascans Content Addendum][ecla].
- **CC0 still requires a credible source.** CC0 removes the affirmer's copyright restrictions to the extent possible; it does not clear others' trademarks, patents or other rights, or guarantee ownership. Avoid ripped game models, unexplained branded vehicles and recognisable third-party characters even if an uploader labels them free. [CC0 §§2–4][cc0].
- **CC BY needs a credits and distribution plan.** Keep the creator and supplied notices, source URL, licence link and modification note. Do not wrap the licensed material in additional contractual/technical restrictions that block the recipient's CC rights; review the game's packaging/EULA, rather than assuming any encryption is automatically fine or automatically forbidden. [CC BY 4.0 §§2–3][ccby]. A proposed record is: `asset title; creator; source URL; CC BY 4.0 URL; modifications; supplied notices`.
- **NC, ND and SA are not synonyms for BY.** NC excludes the commercial use intended here; ND constrains sharing adaptations; SA adds obligations and can conflict with proprietary asset terms. For a low-restriction shortlist, exclude them unless separately reviewed, rather than declaring all Creative Commons assets interchangeable. See [NC legal text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en), [ND legal text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/legalcode.en), [SA legal text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), and [Fab EULA §6(a)][fab].
- **A Blender export does not cleanse the inputs.** Original Blender output can be commercial and need not be GPL, but imported textures/meshes retain their conditions. An add-on's software licence does not necessarily describe its bundled artwork. [Blender licence statement][blender-license], [Fab EULA §§3–6][fab], [CC BY §3][ccby].

### Proposed acquisition record and acceptance gate

When the asset pass is authorised, the lead should maintain a per-item manifest containing: source URL, creator/publisher, exact asset/version, date acquired, licence name/version and saved text, tier/receipt, AI flag, attribution text, modifications, permitted source-sharing boundary, and technical test results. Keep receipt/account details privately; the research file needs no personal information.

Before bulk purchase/import, validate **one tree, building and boat** against the existing factory and camera range. Check: 5.7 import and cook; no unwanted plugin dependency; scale/pivots/bounds; simple collision; material-slot count and texture memory; LOD silhouettes; wind and shadow behaviour; wet/storm/night appearance; construction-state strategy; and representative 3060 GPU cost. A store compatibility badge passes none of those runtime checks by itself.

Questions for Claude's review: select the first culture/age for the close-up quality test; decide whether Standard/NoAI assets are acceptable in the planned tooling; establish the eligible price tier privately; and assign the separate character/wildlife art scope. None blocks using this document to choose an evaluation route. No asset acquisition or implementation is requested by this PR.
