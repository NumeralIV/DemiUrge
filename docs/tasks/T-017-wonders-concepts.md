# T-017 — Concept art: one wonder per age

**Assignee:** Grok · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/grok/T-017`
**Deliver to:** `docs/concept/wonders/` only.

## Why
Wonders are the landmarks a people leaves on the planet, visible from high up and remembered in
the chronicle. Your T-002 gave two wonders; the game needs one recognisable wonder for each age.

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md`; `docs/SPEC-SIM.md` section 1 (`BUILDINGS`, wonder) and 3.8
(prophets and faith); your `docs/concept/landmarks/` (wonder-sunwell, wonder-stair-of-rains) and
`docs/concept/settlements/` (each age's look and the three culture styles).

## Deliver (high oblique view from about 1 km, golden hour, set in its city of that age)
1. Seven wonders, one per age, invented (no real-world monuments): `wonder-0-stone.png` …
   `wonder-6-space.png`. Each should make sense as a people's gift or answer to their god.
2. `wonders-from-orbit.png`: the planet from about 400 km with two or three wonders still
   readable as distinctive marks (scale, light or shape).
3. `index.md`: per wonder, its name (in the voice of `docs/content/chronicle-voice.md`), what it
   is for, the prompt, dimensions in metres, and notes for a modeller (silhouette, materials, hex
   colours, how it is lit at night).

## Acceptance criteria
Invented and distinct from each other; each fits its age and city; readable at game distance;
cinematic and near-photoreal; no text or watermarks.
