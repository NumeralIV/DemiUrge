# T-010 — Concept art: the genesis sequence from orbit

**Assignee:** Grok · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/grok/T-010`
**Deliver to:** `docs/concept/genesis/` only.

## Why
The first five minutes (genesis) are the player's first impression and the tutorial. The sky and
effects modules need a target for how the planet looks at each step.

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md` ("Flow of a game": molten, water, life, people);
`docs/SPEC-SIM.md` section 3.12 (phases) and the genesis rules in 3.3 (climate);
`shots/ue-cloudlab-terminator-final.png` (the approved orbit look) and your own
`docs/concept/ui/screen-genesis.png`.

## Deliver (all from orbit, the whole planet in frame, same planet and continents throughout)
1. `genesis-1-molten.png`: a glowing molten world, crust plates with lava seams, no water.
2. `genesis-2-cooling.png`: the crust dark and cooling, first steam clouds.
3. `genesis-3-comets.png`: comets streaking in and striking; the first seas pooling in basins.
4. `genesis-4-seas.png`: oceans filled, bare rock continents, a thin young atmosphere.
5. `genesis-5-life-wave.png`: green spreading outward over a continent from one touch point, as a
   visible wave front.
6. `genesis-6-awakening.png`: a living world at dusk with the first tiny fire lights where the first
   people wake.
7. `index.md`: per image, what it shows, the prompt, and notes for the sky and effects engineers
   (colours as hex, glow intensity, how the change between steps should animate).

## Acceptance criteria
Recognisably the same planet in all six; each step reads instantly from orbit; cinematic and
near-photoreal; no text or watermarks.
