# T-006 — Design review of the game rules

**Assignee:** any helper (Astra first; Astra is out of usage, so another helper may take it) · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/astra/T-006`
**Deliver to:** `docs/reviews/astra-design-review.md` only.

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md`; `docs/SPEC-SIM.md` in full; `docs/SPEC-UI.md` sections 4–5.

## Deliver
A player-focused review. Is a 30–40 minute history fun to watch and to influence? Where will the
player be bored, confused or powerless? Do the powers, the Awe economy and the endings create
interesting choices? Which balance targets (SPEC-SIM section 4) look wrong? For each finding give
the problem, the spec section, a concrete proposed change, and how it could be tested in a
headless simulation run. Rank findings by impact, and close with the five changes you would make first.

## Acceptance criteria
Specific, actionable and tied to spec sections; do not rewrite the specs themselves.
