# T-005 — Chronicle voice: sample entries and god epithets

**Assignee:** Grok · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/grok/T-005`
**Deliver to:** `docs/content/chronicle-voice.md` only (a proposal).

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md` (tone: reads like a translated ancient text; mythic, warm, a
little wry); `docs/SPEC-SIM.md` sections 2 (events) and 3.11 (chronicle).

## Deliver
1. 60 sample chronicle entries covering the event types in SPEC-SIM section 2 (foundings,
   famines, wars, prophets, miracles, disasters, ages reached, the god's acts), with the
   placeholders the game fills, written like
   `In the {year}th year of the {culture}, {god_epithet} ...`. Give three variants for the most
   common events so entries never repeat.
2. 40 god epithets grouped by how the god behaves (kind, cruel, chaotic, absent, rain-giving,
   fire-throwing, …), in the spirit of "the Rain-Giver" or "She Who Burns the Sky".
3. Five ending passages: beloved, feared, forgotten, silence, and one of your design.
4. A style guide of about ten rules that keep the voice consistent.

## Acceptance criteria
Original prose; varied rhythm; no modern slang; every placeholder listed in a table at the top.
