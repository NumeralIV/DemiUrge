# T-002 — Concept art: landmarks, wonders and god powers

**Assignee:** Grok · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/grok/T-002`
**Deliver to:** `docs/concept/landmarks/` only.

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md` (powers list); `docs/SPEC-SIM.md` section 1 (`BUILDINGS`) and
section 3.10 (powers and disasters); `docs/SPEC-RENDER.md` section 10 (effects).

## Deliver
1. Buildings, one image each, same camera rules as T-001: shrine, temple, granary, walls, port,
   market, mine, factory, academy, power plant, launch site, and two wonders of your design.
2. God powers and disasters as the player sees them (from 2–20 km up, cinematic): comet strike on
   the sea, meteor impact on land, volcano eruption building an island, lightning storm with a
   forest fire, blessing light on a town, plague over a city, the first ships leaving for the stars.
3. `index.md` as in T-001 (file, what it shows, prompt, notes for artists and effects engineers).

## Acceptance criteria
As T-001. Effects must stay readable at a distance: clear shapes, light and colour, not noise.
