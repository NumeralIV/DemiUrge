# T-016 — Concept art: the night side through the ages

**Assignee:** Grok · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/grok/T-016`
**Deliver to:** `docs/concept/night/` only.

## Why
City lights on the night side are one of the game's signature sights: from orbit the player watches
civilisation spread as light. The planet module draws them from settlement data; it needs a target
for how they look in each age.

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md` (pillar 1); `shots/ue-cloudlab-terminator-final.png`; your
`docs/concept/world-states/` (same planet and framing) and `docs/concept/settlements/city-night.png`.

## Deliver (whole planet from orbit, same planet and framing as T-013, terminator in view)
1. `night-stone.png`: a few scattered firelights (warm orange pinpricks).
2. `night-iron.png`: town clusters and the first lit roads.
3. `night-industry.png`: dense yellow city lights, rail lines, coastal ports, some haze.
4. `night-space.png`: networks of cities joined by lit corridors, a launch site glow.
5. `night-aurora.png`: the night side with a polar aurora and moonlit clouds.
6. `index.md`: per image, the prompt and notes for the engineer: light colour temperatures (hex),
   brightness relative to the day side, how lights cluster (city size versus brightness), roads
   and coasts, and how clouds dim them.

## Acceptance criteria
Same planet and coastlines as T-013; the age progression reads from the lights alone; cinematic and
near-photoreal; no text or watermarks.
