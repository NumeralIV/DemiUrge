# T-004 — Culture and language design proposals

**Assignee:** any helper (Astra first; Astra is out of usage, so another helper may take it) · **Status:** open · **Branch:** `helper/<your-name>/T-004`
**Deliver to:** `docs/content/cultures.md` only (a proposal; the lead folds accepted parts into the build).

## Read first
`AGENTS.md`; `docs/CONCEPT.md`; `docs/SPEC-SIM.md` sections 1, 3.1 (names) and 3.8 (society).
Names are generated from per-culture phonologies, and cultures split off from each other over history.

## Deliver
Eight invented culture families. For each: a one-paragraph identity (environment it suits,
values, how it might regard a god); a phonology (consonant and vowel inventories, syllable
shapes, stress, forbidden clusters); naming patterns for places, people, rulers, god epithets and
ships, with 15 examples of each kind; architecture and colour notes; and how a daughter culture's
sound drifts from its parent's. Finish with a short section on keeping names pronounceable and
distinct between families.

## Acceptance criteria
Invented, not copied from real languages or existing fiction; each family recognisable by its
place names alone; written so a programmer can turn it into tables.
