LENS: player
VERDICT: Not ready for a parallel build from the player-experience lens. Two gaps will break the game as designed: touch has no way to disarm a power, open the
menu or un-hide the HUD, and the witness/devotion scale that drives the whole Awe loop is undefined between two owners. Beyond those, the Awe income
formula overshoots its own target by 10 to 40 times from the mid-game, the unlock schedule and hotkeys do not exist, the phone camera and HUD do not
fit by arithmetic, and the player is never shown a goal. Nearly every fix is a paste-in rule, table or small API, so one revision pass before the
build starts should be enough; the specs are otherwise coherent and the genesis flow, chronicle and ending structure are strong.
