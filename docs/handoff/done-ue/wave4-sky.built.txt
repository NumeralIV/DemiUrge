ROOT CAUSES FOUND
1. Night "cream daylight" round a city (perf-ab night, release-notes 1a) is NOT moonlight or the sky light. It is the planet material's ground city glow (Scripts/planet/land.hlsl, "lightsK ... * 3.5", clump noise at 1,400 m / 450 m): with the moonlight switched off (-moonnearlux=0, shots/ue-s5c-night-nomoon.png) the camouflage pattern stays (luma 66), and it is unchanged by clouds (r.VolumetricCloud=0), Lumen and nature (ue-s5b-night-*.png, all luma 92.5) and by the exposure lift (1.0 vs 0.3 EV: luma 95 vs 98, the glow is clipped). The sky made it CREAM: its night grade (blue scene tint 0.70/0.84/1.16, -40 % saturation, -700 K white point) bleached the orange glow. FOR THE PLANET OWNER (planet4): fade the ground glow out near the ground (today it keeps 15 % below 40 m footprints and 100 % above; at a 2 km camera it is still several times brighter than moonlit land); suggested: multiply lightsK by saturate((fp - 60) / 400) or scale its 3.5 gain by camera distance, and break the clump noise along roads / blocks instead of 1 km blobs.
2. No cyclone on screen in sky-cyclone / sky-cyclone-top (ue-s5a-cyclone*.png, ue-s5d-cyclone*.png) and sky-check "weather maps rebuilt (0 new)": the weather map build never finished. -skytrace showed "maps 0 pending 1" for every frame: the job ran in the shared thread pool behind the planet's / towns' jobs (and its ParallelFor runs at background priority), so the maps stayed at the start-up build, in which the sim storm had strength 0. In live play this freezes the weather and the storms whenever the pool is busy.
3. The soft grey blobs on the hurricane's overcast (ue-s5n-cyclone-close.png) are FX quads, not clouds: gone with -noparts=fx (ue-s5p-nofx-cyclone-close.png). FOR THE FX OWNER (fx4b): the storm effect's quads read as grey discs from 60-250 km by day.
4. Every exit was killed 60 s after quitting in two runs (s5i, s5j): "DmTowns: joined 12 planning tasks in 48320 ms" in EndPlay on the loaded machine. FOR THE TOWNS OWNER: do not join planning tasks at exit (same pattern as wave3-final fix 6).

CHANGES (all in Game/Sky and Scripts/sky)
a) Weather maps on their own thread (AsyncThread, TPri_SlightlyBelowNormal = OS normal; the ParallelFor still borrows idle workers). -skywxpool restores the pool. Result: sky-check "weather maps rebuilt (2 new)", first map 2.7 s after start on the loaded machine.
b) Night near the ground (below ~8 km, fades out by 60 km; orbit night unchanged): the moonlight is a fixed moderate key (MoonNearLux 2, x0.75 at new moon .. x1 at full) from at least 40 deg above the ground in view (the first attempt's work: one view was brighter than day, another pitch black), coloured blue (0.52, 0.68, 1.0; -mooncolor=); exposure lift 1 EV near the ground (2.6 EV from orbit; -nightevnear=); no scene tint; saturation -10 % (was -40 %); white point -250 K (was -700 K); split toning of the SHADOWS only (sat 0.45, gain 0.76/0.92/1.34, shadows max 0.12; -nighttone=0 to compare), so the moonlit land is blue-black and the city glow and windows stay warm. Thin cloud ambient x(1 + 3) at night near the ground (-cloudnight=).
c) Clouds (first attempt, kept and now baked into M_SkyClouds): cubic B-spline lookup of the cell texture within mip 0 (round cloud edges instead of 150 m texel stairs; -finecubic=0 for the old bilinear); cloud shadow map spatial filtering 3 passes (-cloudshadowfilter=); cloud shadow on surface 0.85 (shade, not navy holes). Static planet frame: the planet no longer turns in the world; the sun, moon, stars (M_SkyStars StarYaw) and aurora turn round it (-skyturn restores). Reason: the volumetric clouds' temporal reconstruction and the world-snapped cloud shadow map lost their history every frame while the planet turned (dithered edges, crawling shadows), and every primitive was re-transformed per frame. GetPlanetToWorld() is now the identity unless -skyturn; GetDayAngle() gives the time-of-day angle. sky-check verifies the identity and the turning sun.
d) Storm eye (DmSkyWeather Tropical): soft ramp 0.05-0.16 R (was a hard step at 0.05-0.085 R), wall at 0.16 R, the overcast top sloping into it (stadium 0.3 -> 1 from 0.06 to 0.45 R) and a floor of scattered low cloud (cover up to 0.55, tops at about a third of the layer) so the eye shades grey instead of punching a black hole. From 250 km it is now a small grey dimple (ue-s5n-cyclone.png, like docs/concept/world-states/state-warming.png); close up a partly filled eye (ue-s5n-cyclone-close.png); at dusk still a dark pit in the wall's shadow, smaller (ue-s5n-cyclone-dusk.png).
e) Lightning glow discs: night and dusk only, and only over cloud (cover >= 0.25-0.5): by day they drew white discs over clear sea and the overcast. Night lightning still shows (ue-s5n-night.png).
f) Scenarios / tools: sky-look views cyclone, cyclone-dusk, cyclone-close (framed on the world's storm, grown at once); FDmSkyStorms::SettleSimStorms() (scenarios and sky-check use it so a shot or check does not catch a storm still forming); sky-check waits for two maps after the storm is settled; storm check is now "thin eye inside its overcast" (eye < 0.6 and overcast > eye + 0.25: the eye has a cloud floor by design); -skytrace also logs the look every 120 frames.
