Not met (v1 targets), with the reason
- Cadence with emergent off:
  - Passive gets 5-11 cards per 3,600 years and none in Atomic or Space; the prayers family fixes the count.
  - "Every age of >= 200 years opens a card": Space (about 220 years) often has no proposal.
  - "No auto-paced opening within 1 minute of another" conflicts with minGap 70, as M9 found. To hold it, a gap must
    be at least 240 years minus the years the card stays open, which also breaks the 150-260 median.
- kingmaker never chooses and passive has 0 competing-prayer cards: both need the prayers family. With it on,
  kingmaker's culture launches the Ark on 1-3/8.
- exact-only agency is 0.45-0.55 against <= 0.25 (obedience 0.62-0.65). An Exact word costs 60, or 120 from the sky,
  so the policy can afford about one per people per 150 years, and clarity fades back toward 0.5 in between.
- judge-deeds vs judge-hearts, and stern vs merciful, are within 0.02. The targets are people-weighted over the
  whole world, but judgements come 2-13 per history across 6+ peoples (the director's cultureGap and budget), so
  doctrine reaches only the judged peoples.
- stern ends forgotten, and unseen-kind ends beloved (Society's beloved-by-prayers rule answers the kind half).
- Lawgiver is Obedient 0/8. Its decree set includes Shelter, so the Compassionate score (about 0.66) beats the
  Obedient score, which is capped near 0.55 without House authority (v2).
- Rival Ways and the Unbelieved's vindication need Faiths and Omens (v2).
