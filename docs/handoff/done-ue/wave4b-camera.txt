FOUND AND FIXED (Game/Camera, lane camera4b-verify):
F1 Zoom toward the pointer lost its point near the limb / at grazing angles. This was a real defect.
   Cause, part 1: every wheel notch re-anchored on whatever ground the pointer covered at that moment. Any residual
   left by the previous notch was baked in.
   Cause, part 2: the per-frame correction budget (8 x |zoom step|) is too small where the ground is seen edge-on.
   Effect: zooming from orbit at a point near the limb, the point under the pointer ended 700-900 px away at 11 km
   (break-v1 / -v3 runs). The rig's own anchor residual measured 1-6 px per notch at orbit, magnified 30x on the way
   down.
   Fix (DmCameraRig.cpp ZoomBy / Update):
   - A notch that lands while an anchor is still held under the same pixel (< 4 px) keeps that anchor.
   - The correction budget is divided by the cosine of the pointer ray's incidence (clamped at 0.12).
   Result: limb drift 895 px -> 3.4 px. The normal cases are unchanged (wheel-in 0.0 px, pad 1.4 px, peak 0.0 px).
F2 The proof's drift reference was wrong at grazing angles (DmCameraRecord.cpp AimPointer).
   - The 4-pass sphere iteration does not converge at the limb or the horizon. It put the reference point above or
     below the ground, which reads as false drift through parallax.
   - It is now the game's own pick when the player view already equals the rig view. Otherwise (right after a
     jump, when the camera manager is a frame stale) it is a convergent march (half-height steps plus bisection)
     against the camera's ground.
   - A first attempt that always used the pick was itself wrong right after JumpLatLon: the camera manager is one
     frame stale, which gave a false 516 px at peak-in. That is why the hybrid exists.
F3 New verifier coverage in DmCameraRecord.cpp:
   - -camset=break and -camset=limb;
   - CSV columns anchor and anchor_err (the rig's own anchor residual in px);
   - a limb-hold phase that logs planet StatsLine and the rig-vs-camera-manager eye gap (0.000 km).
