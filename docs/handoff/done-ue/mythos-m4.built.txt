Built (Sim/Characters.cpp replaced in full; header unchanged, no header change needed)
- candidates(w, sid): three distinct archetypes per town. The Criminal is offered only in a town or larger. The
  Queen is offered only in the capital, or in a town or larger of a culture that has a living ruler. The draw is a
  pure read forked from Rng(w.seed) by "myth:characters:candidates:<sid>:<decade>". w.rng's state moves every tick,
  so a w.rng fork could not show the same three people when the power is armed again in the same decade. Names
  come from Names (names::person, names::epithet), with non-empty fallbacks. Person and hidden heart are biased by
  archetype. Credibility starts at the archetype's value. Reach is the archetype's rural / town / court / outcast
  row weighted by where the culture's people live.
- choose(w, sid, i) creates the Messenger figure, then calls society::raiseProphet(w, sid, { figure }). If that is
  refused, the figure is removed and nextId is restored. At the choice it forks the risks: the Criminal relapses at
  20 % within 30 years, the Queen shows pride at 25 %. The Queen adds House authority +0.1 through
  faiths::adjustHouse. Emits figure chosen.
- yearly (phase >= 1; mythos::yearly calls it). Messengers:
  - A messenger dies when the culture's Culture::prophet no longer names the figure.
  - Credibility: +0.05 per kind of miracle per year in the home or current town (x1.5 under Messengers); -0.1 for
    wrath the messenger did not foretell (no Warning word through them in 30 years); a 1 % drift toward the
    archetype's start; the Child +0.01 a year. Acts are read from MythosState::acts in [last pass, this year).
  - Criminal: redeemed by a miracle within 20 years (+0.4, flag, figure redeemed; then the House's corruption falls
    0.01 a year through adjustHouse). If not redeemed, relapses at the forked year (Relapsed and Discredited flags,
    figure discredited).
  - Queen: pride shows at the forked year (Proud flag).
  - Child: martyred when the home town is taken (culture changed), razed in war, or struck by the god. The fate is
    Martyred; legends::add asks for the martyr legend; society::nudge lowers love by 0.1 in the town when the god
    struck it.
  - Relapse and pride become a standing deed. A Judgement proposal is made each year for up to 30 years until a
    Judgement card about that figure opens.
- yearly, rulers: exactly one living ruler per living Bronze+ culture whose capital is alive and its own. Each reign
  is forked to 15-45 years. On a natural end an heir follows (75 %, same house); otherwise a new house. A new house
  always follows when the capital fell, was taken or moved. The ruler is removed when the culture loses its capital
  or dies. Names come from names::ruler and names::epithet; an heir inherits half of the person traits. Emits
  figure rise / died.
- yearly, "they seek a voice": a Bronze+ culture with no voice for 250 years (counted from its birth or the last
  year a prophet or messenger lived) and a population-weighted devotion of at least 0.45 proposes MessengerChoice.
  It does not propose while a MessengerChoice card is open for that culture.
- adjustCredibility (clamped 0..1): an Unbelieved messenger reaching 0.8 is vindicated, even after death. Effects:
  Vindicated flag, data quoted = year + 1000, figure vindicated (once), legends::add.
- spawn: for Faiths' rival voices. Validates culture, home and kind; fills a missing name; sanitises NaN traits;
  emits figure rise.
- fell: sets fate and died year and emits figure died (Died) or fallen (other fates). A removed ruler is succeeded
  in the same call by a new house.
- The MessengerChoice card handlers:
  - options: choices 0-2 cost card.data["cost"] if set (askCost 60 on a "seek a voice" card), else the prophet
    power's 120. A choice is marked Awe when the god cannot pay, and Unavailable when the culture already has a
    voice or the candidate is missing. Choice 3 (none) is free.
  - answer: uses the candidates of the decade the card opened in (data["decade"], else floor(opened / 10)).
  - lapse: nothing (none).
- Registry cap 160: when full, the longest-dead figure that nothing names is dropped. "Names" means prophets,
  decrees, words, pacts, cards, rival voices, omens or healer towns. A vindicated voice still being quoted is kept.
  Ids are always below nextId, so the save's MYTH validation holds.
- Phase 0: candidates, choose, spawn, fell, adjustCredibility, answer and options are neutral, and yearly does
  nothing. ruler, messenger, credibility and figure stay honest reads.
