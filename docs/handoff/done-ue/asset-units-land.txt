2026-11-19, asset-units-land (family Unit, art/units-land) verified by an independent asset verifier.

Reproducibility. The family was rebuilt twice from build_units-land.py through tools/render_slot.py. The textures and the manifest were byte-identical, and the FBX files differed only in the FBX timestamp. The verifier then re-imported all 21 FBX files into an empty Blender scene:
- Hierarchy is a LodGroup empty with _LOD0 to _LOD3, and every triangle count matches the manifest.
- Scale is 1.0 in metres. Each LOD has the three slots M_Unit_Structure, M_Unit_Roof and M_Unit_Detail in that order.
- UVs are inside 0-1, and there are no n-gons.
- Pivots: every footprint is centred on 0,0, with the ground at z 0 and the aprons at -0.3. Every mesh faces -Y.
- Normals were checked after welding, and no closed part is inverted.
- The 1.8 m figure checks out: people are 1.6-1.76 m, and the oxen, camels, horses, buses, trucks, aircraft and the 101 m ark are all to plausible scale.
- Every unit is within the vehicle budget of 1-4k (largest 3,910), and the ark is 4.9k. Names follow docs/ASSETS.md. The manifest has every required field, plus sim mapping and suggested_unit_scale.

Fixes, all made in the script and recorded in the README:
1. The preview rig's 1.8 m reference figure stood 1.56 m underground with only its head showing, so the builder's scale check could not have been seen. It now stands on the ground in every 40 m view.
2. Fanned bus-end caps produced 12 zero-area triangles. They are dropped, and the manifest now counts the triangles of the exported mesh.
3. The track strip had hidden end faces at every 4 m segment (30 non-manifold edges). Only the strip ends are capped now.
4. Truck column LOD1 was 62 % of LOD0. Glass, lamps and the middle axle are now LOD0 only, which brings LOD1 to 39 %.
5. The damaged ark's breach was a see-through hole. It now has a scorched inner wall, plus the smoke plume that its description promised.
6. On the launch, the smoke column hid the plume, so the rocket seemed to float over a cloud. About 11 m of white-gold plume now shows.
7. Manifest text: the ring glow is 0.12 (it said 0.35), and the aircraft entries now give the ground ring's x1.9 scale.

The builder also said that only the ark reports inward faces. That is not true: one tail end cap each in Settlers_Ancient and Traders_Ancient also trips the heuristic. Both are false positives, and the README now documents them.

All 63 previews were re-rendered (shots/asset-units-land-*-{40m,300m,1km}.png) and judged against docs/concept/units/units-silhouettes.png and the age images:
- Every unit type keeps its shape per age: the settler column with a cart, wagon or bus; traders as a line of bright loads; the army as the only type with spikes and banners; the white prophet with a gold halo; space craft light on dark, with the army craft dark.
- The culture ring and the culture loads, pennants and banners read in all six colours at 1 km.

Open for others:
- The FBX LodGroup has to be imported with Import Mesh LODs on, and that is unconfirmed until the first Unreal import. art/tools/ue_import.py expects one FBX per LOD under art/export/, so it cannot import this family as it is.
- The integration agent must wire the culture tint (six MI_Unit_Detail_* instances or per-instance colour) and the per-mesh suggested_unit_scale.
- Art limits: the figures and animals are mannequin-grade low-poly. They read at game scale but not in close-ups, which is a long way from near-photoreal. The ark's gantry is a rust-brown lattice that hides much of the hull, there is no crawler convoy, and the aircraft and small craft keep 55-70 % at LOD1 (all under 600 triangles).
