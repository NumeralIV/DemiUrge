Decisions where the spec leaves the C++ open (the lead may move them)
- Why the migration waits for the god's first mythos act: M1's test_mythos_guard compares the version-1 save bytes
  (the chronicle is in them) of a phase-1 and a phase-0 history with an ordinary god, which is stricter than the
  5.1 fingerprint; any word changed at phase 1 for a god who ignores the mythos would break it. So a phase-1 world
  whose god never touches the mythos keeps today's words; the lint's six gods all use the mythos. If the lead wants
  the neutral words for every phase-1 world, mythVoice() is the one switch, and test_mythos_guard would then need
  to compare the sim fields instead of the whole save.
