summary:
- Reproducible: rebuilt from art/age5/build_age5.py --textures (headless, factory startup, about 1 minute).
  The builder's numbers came back exactly (100 meshes, 398,223 LOD0 triangles). After the fixes, two consecutive
  builds produced byte-identical textures and the same manifest apart from its date.
- Checked on all 100 FBX files and all 400 LOD meshes after re-import into an empty Blender scene:
  - an FBX LodGroup with four meshes named _LOD0.._LOD3;
  - three slots in the order M_Age5_Structure / Roof / Detail on every LOD;
  - triangle counts and bounds equal to manifest.json;
  - no n-gons, zero-area faces or duplicated faces;
  - positive signed volume (normals out);
  - one UV map plus the Col attribute;
  - every facade face inside one trim strip;
  - Z up, front -Y;
  - pivot at the footprint centre, with buildings reaching Z = -0.3 or lower;
  - SM_/M_/MI_/T_ naming;
  - every LOD0 within budget (largest: OfficeGlass_High_Damaged, 16.2k of 20k).
- Scale: checked against a 1.8 m figure at exactly 40 m, using a new "close" preview view. Storeys are 4.2 m for
  shops, 3.0 m for flats and 3.6 m for offices. Towers are 10-32 storeys, the cooling tower is 112 m and the pylon
  44 m.
- Totals after fixes: 424,336 triangles at LOD0, 779,622 across all LODs.

fixes (all in art/age5/, recorded in art/age5/README.md "Independent verification"):
  1. Sockets: the FBX and the manifest carried Blender-suffixed names (SOCKET_Crown_1.033, SOCKET_WarnLight_1.018,
     ...), so every mesh had a differently named crown socket. They now export as SOCKET_<Kind>_<n>.
  2. LOD group: the root Empty was a plain Null. It is now an FBX LodGroup (fbx_type), as in age0-age4. The builder's
     "Blender cannot write a LOD group" gap is closed.
  3. University lecture hall: the east gable triangle had sheared UVs (244 m per UV). A duplicate coplanar triangle
     would have z-fought.
  4. Damage and ruin read as stacked dark voxel cubes. Every broken interior wall now gets a pale torn floor-slab lip
     at its storey line, so blast holes read as exposed floors (+26k LOD0 triangles across the family).
  5. Pylon footings now reach Z = -0.3 (they stopped at -0.1).
  6. Plant tank roofs no longer show the steel row's yellow pie wedges (they use pale concrete).
  7. Highway asphalt lightened from #4a4946 to #5f5d58, so highways read as the concept's pale ribbons.

previews (all rendered through tools/render_slot.py):

against the concept:

  - age-5-atomic.png: at 300 m-1 km the district gives the grey and beige mid-century downtown, with flat roofs,

held back from a higher score:
  - Materials are clean and evenly lit. Weathering, rain streaks and roof variety are thin, so close up it is
    "good game kit" rather than near-photoreal.
  - Damage is still cell-based: blast edges are stepped, and the ruins' tall remnant is a stack of blocks.
  - The reactor dome uses the same pale concrete as the rest; the concept's dome is whiter (raise its MI tint).
  - Windows are texture only (no reveals) at 40 m.
  - LOD1 is 50-80 % of LOD0 on the cheap towers.
  - The interchange's four outer ramps close into a near-full ring: a stylised cloverleaf.
