Budget: token buckets that refill per decade. Story entries (epithets, the first age, peoples born / dead, wonders, the Ark, the god's meteors / volcanoes / quakes, climate, genesis, endings) are never dropped or demoted. Tellable entries (towns destroyed, war / peace, later ages, prophets, edicts, the god's heal / inspire / omen / plague) are never dropped: notable while the story bucket lasts (0.3 a decade, burst 4), then minor; a fallen city is epochal while the epochal bucket lasts (0.08 a decade, burst 2: about 1 a real minute). Routine notables (city famines, growth, first buildings, answered prayers, captures, trade) are notable while the notable bucket lasts (0.25 a decade, burst 3), then minor. An answered prayer is notable only the first time a people is answered in each age.
Digests: a routine minor entry is written only if it is the first of its type for its people this decade, the world's minor bucket has a token (2.5 a decade, burst 6; the god's own small acts are exempt), and its template family has a line under templateCap (10). Otherwise it is folded into the decade's ledger; the next decade's first entry (or the yearly updateEpithets) writes one digest: "That decade Sarello answered the prayers of seven towns of the Domafi, and they raised twelve new buildings" (counts in words, "they" for the same people; a clause needs count x the kind's weight >= digestMin 5). Templates are picked least-used first (counts in vars.num), the family's first (voice) line opens it.
