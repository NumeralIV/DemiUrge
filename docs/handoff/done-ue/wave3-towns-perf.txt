FOUND AND FIXED (all in Game/Towns)
1. The verdict depended on luck (DmTownsPerf). Towns time was wall time, so an OS stall inside the towns tick counted as towns work. On main's code, 2 of 4 city runs FAILED: 2 and 4 frames attributed, towns tick 23.6 and 52.1 ms. The cause was always a phase with nothing to wait on, e.g. the units loop over 29 units, or moving the actor.
   - Fix: the game thread's CPU time is now measured per towns phase (QueryThreadCycleTime, calibrated against the TSC at start), and frames are attributed by CPU time.
   - Example: a "units 97.9 ms" frame used 0.42 ms of CPU. Over 13 runs there were 1-14 such stalls per run, spread over follow / units / signatures / apply / construction. Towns CPU never went over 5 ms in any of them.
   - The wall-time count ("by wall time N") and every stall are still logged.
2. An in-view settlement kept an outdated plan for 90 s (savanna, two runs: settlement 88, a tier 4 metropolis; dirty, not pending, last plan 96 s ago).
   - Cause: one shared limit of 6 pending plans. Six out-of-view plans on below-normal-priority workers held all the slots for minutes on a busy machine, so nothing in view could launch.
   - Fix (LaunchPlans): in-view settlements have their own 3 foreground slots. A settlement that comes into view while its plan waits on a background worker is re-planned on a foreground one; the old task is orphaned and cancelled if it has not started.
   - After: 0 in-view settlements unplanned or out of date in city, savanna, forest and herd (8 runs).
3. Coastal plans could not land while the sea rose.
   - Cause: after the age change at 8x the ground job ran every ~1.3 s and "moved" 40-94 of ~100 settlements each time, mostly by the sea. A plan whose ground moved again while it was being made (1-3 s) was thrown away and re-planned, so coastal towns could loop forever at ~1 s of worker time per plan.
   - Fix: such a plan is applied (it is one ground snapshot old, newer than what is drawn) and the town stays dirty. A ground-only re-plan waits at least 6 s after the last plan (-townground=); structural changes are not delayed.
   - This is a logic fix. Its worker-time saving was not isolated by measurement on this machine.
4. A game was killed at exit: the herd run "did not exit 60 s after it asked to quit". Towns EndPlay waited for up to 15 queued background plans.
   - Fix: each plan task has a cancel flag set by JoinAll, so plans not yet started return at once.
   - After: "joined 9-13 planning tasks in 0.4-0.5 s" in normal runs, 12.5 s once on the saturated machine (plans already running finish). No more kills in 7 runs.
5. New coverage check in towns-hitch: unplanned and out-of-date settlements (over 8 s, in view = failure), with a state dump. The old check only compared drawn settlements with their plans. A settlement with no plan, or an outdated one, passed it.
6. The units update logs its breakdown when it takes over 3 ms (loop vs components; it is always the loop).
