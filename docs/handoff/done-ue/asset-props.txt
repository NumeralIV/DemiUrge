- Fixes, all in art/props/ and recorded in its README (Verification section):
  1. LODs were folded into sheets. Collapse-decimating LOD0 straight to 12 % left duplicate-face sheets in 53 of
     the 199 LOD meshes, so the manifest triangle counts did not match the FBX for 34 meshes. The worst cases:
     - Fence_DryStone LOD2: all 94 stones had become flaps;
     - Hay_Stack LOD2: only the pole and slivers were left;
     - Crate_Stack LOD2: the crates were sheets.
     The fix:
     - LOD k is built from gen(k), and a decimation is accepted only if it keeps the bounding box, the surface
       area and the silhouette;
     - clean_mesh deletes duplicate faces and loose vertices;
     - native LOD2s for the dry-stone wall (a wall hull), crates and the hay rick;
     - no LOD is heavier than the one before.
  2. Loose vertices in 12 LOD meshes (decimated LOD2s, and the torn awnings of the damaged and burnt stalls).
  3. Cliff_B LOD3 equalled LOD2 (256); it is now 210 (decimated in a chain).
  4. Rock and cliff fissures read as black inked cells (a "giraffe" pattern on the cliffs at 40 m). The cliffs now
     have vertical joints, half as deep, and each rock's cracks follow one direction; all 12 sets were re-baked.
  5. The Crop_Vine canopy was an ellipsoid tapering at the tile ends, so tiled rows read as sausage links every
     4 m. It is now a continuous, seamless row.
  6. Crop_Veg LOD2 was 360 triangles per 4 m tile; it is now 120.
  7. Barrel_Stack (7 mm) and Hedge_A (5 mm) floated above the ground.
  8. Lighter lamp and totem LODs.
  9. props_preview.py gained the verifier scenes.
