FOUND AND FIXED (Config/DefaultScalability.ini, Game/Core/DmPerfScenarios.cpp)
1. High was NOT identical to Epic, though the file and report said it was. The engine's base file still set 16 keys differently at level 2, among them r.SceneColorFormat (3 = R11G11B10 at High, 4 = RGBA16F at Epic), r.DetailMode 1 vs 3, the DOF kernels, heterogeneous volumes, grass and emitter density. So the settings screen's "High" and "Auto" were two different looks. I wrote them out with Epic's values at levels 2 and 3, and with explicit values at 0 and 1. Some keys were set by one level and left alone by others, which made the result depend on the previous tier. Cost: the scene colour format A/B (scf3 vs base) is within +/-0.2 ms in all six views, and the pictures differ by less than 1.3 grey levels on average.
2. New regression check: shot.ps1 perf-tiers -noshot, ending "DmTiers RESULT PASS". It checks:
   - all 16 ordered tier pairs give the right resolution (Low 50, Medium 62, High / Epic the display default) and the right GetTier();
   - the settings screen's path Low -> Auto -> High (non-forced levels);
   - every tier gives the same cvars whichever tier came before;
   - High == Epic on all 156 scalability cvars, and Medium / Low really differ;
   - -tier= temporary levels switch on and off cleanly.
   Before the fix it FAILED on High == Epic (it listed the 16 keys). After: PASS on the lane, on main, and on the lane with -tier=low. The packaged game with -tier=low applies Low temporarily and leaves GameUserSettings.ini at Epic (sg.* = 3, ResolutionQuality 0).
