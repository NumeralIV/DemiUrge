Changes
- Zoom: a critically damped spring (no velocity step on a wheel notch, no overshoot) with a 1.5 zoom/s speed cap
  instead of exponential smoothing. Height stays exponential in zoom, so a fast spin crosses orbit -> ground in
  ~0.7 s at the same perceived speed (altitude ratio per second) at every altitude.
- Zoom toward the cursor: the anchor is now pinned AFTER the ground smoothing moves the eye (it used to drift
  over rising ground), with a correction budget of 8 x |zoom step| per frame (scales on long frames, leftover
  error fades out instead of snapping). Measured drift of the ground point under the pointer: <= 1.4 px.
- Rendered-ground floor: the rig now also keeps clear of the planet's rendered LOD surface
  (ADmPlanet::SurfaceHeightM, up to a few hundred m above the sim surface), for the hard floor, the altitude and
  one sample of the smoothed eye reference. 2.9 us a query, about 40 a frame.
- Tilt: 72 deg at zoom 1 (was 68), so the descent ends looking across the land at a curved horizon with a band
  of sky. Zoom rate is continuous through the tilt start (zoom 0.35).
- Pan: keyboard and the new edge pan feed a smoothed pan velocity (eases in at 9/s, glides out at 6/s);
  keys pan at 0.75 screen heights/s, Shift x2.5, diagonals normalised. Edge pan: a 6 px band, 0.18 s dwell,
  0.7 screen heights/s; only while the window has focus and no button is held.
- Settings on UDmControlSubsystem: bEdgePan, ZoomSpeedSetting, PanSpeedSetting, bInvertZoom (and switches
  -edgepan= -zoomspeed= -panspeed= -invertzoom=). The settings screen (UI track) still has to show them.
- ADmPawn::LastCameraUs: camera CPU time per frame (router + rig + view).
