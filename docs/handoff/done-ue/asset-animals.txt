fixes (all in art/animals/, recorded in README.md "Verification pass"):
1. Far LODs had lost their silhouette (the real defect: these are the LODs seen from 300 m to 2 km).
   - The quadric collapse flattened thin parts into zero-volume strips, which clean_degenerate then deleted.
   - Grazers and deer had no legs at LOD2 (wildebeest's lowest point 0.49 m up, stag's 0.22 m).
   - The birds had no wings at LOD2 (egret span 0.33 m instead of 1.42 m, starling 0.09 m instead of 0.39 m), so the
     flap shape keys and the UV1 flap were dead.
   - The whale's LOD2 and LOD3 had lost the flippers, and LOD3 had lost the fluke too.
   - Fix (build_animals.py make_lod / lod_keep_weights): the LODs are chained; each one tries plain and
     extremity-protected collapse, with and without symmetry, and keeps the candidate closest to the LOD0 surface.
   - Walkers and birds now go to LOD2 at 20 % (species_animals.py default), not 12 %: at 12 % the legs webbed into
     skirts.
2. Open edges.
   - Every LOD but the fish's had 6-36 duplicate edges on the x = 0 seam, left by the symmetric collapse.
   - clean_degenerate now ends with Mesh.validate; every LOD is closed. A few edges where tubes meet the body are
     shared by three faces (at most 6 per mesh).
3. Bird normal maps.
   - 8 % (starling) and 3 % (egret) of normal texels were tilted past 70 deg, some facing inward, which gave a black
     patch on the egret's wing.
   - Inward normals are now flat, every map is limited to ~70 deg of tilt, and birds keep a third of the baked tilt
     (write_textures).
4. Preview: the bait ball's fish pointed radially (a starburst). They now mill tangentially, as in wildlife-sea.png
   (preview_animals.py).
5. check_animals.py now also fails on:
   - open edges;
   - walkers whose LOD1 or LOD2 has lost its legs;
   - birds or the whale whose LOD1 or LOD2 has lost its span.
6. README integration note corrected (axes, below), and a note added that Unreal drops morph targets on static meshes
   (the shape keys work from the SK_ files).
