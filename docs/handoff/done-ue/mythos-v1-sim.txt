Defects found and fixed
1. A Messenger choice card stacked for every town armed. Arming the Messenger on each of 11 towns of one people opened 11
   MessengerChoice cards; after one choice, the other 10 stayed open, unanswerable, until their 60-year window ran out.
   Fix in Sim/Cards.cpp:
   - cards::open: arming again for the same people replaces that people's open armed choice (the latest arming wins).
   - cards::answer: once a messenger is chosen, the people's other MessengerChoice cards are withdrawn.
   Now 1 card opens and 0 are left over.
2. A budgeted card's window had no upper bound. A source asking for a window of 1e7 years held a director slot for good (1,199
   years, still open). Fix in Sim/Cards.cpp openCard: a director card's window is capped at CardsTune::maxGap (420). The v1
   windows are 15-80 years, so no real card changes.
3. The waking cards never closed. ShapePeople and Presence stayed open "until answered", so a god could:
   - answer ShapePeople in the Iron age and reshape the nature of every root people for free;
   - answer Presence centuries later for a stance change that skips the 100 Awe and the once-per-age rule.
   Fix in Sim/Balance.cpp: both cards now get a window of CardsTune::firstYear (90). Their lapse changes nothing: the people keep
   their rolled nature and today's stance, and a lapse is not a deed, so it touches no people. The client pauses genesis on these
   cards, so a player never sees the window run out. The core hash is unchanged (136 of 140 above; phase1-guardrail 32 of 32).
4. The stern script did not test what the spec compares. stern played a passive god while merciful played the kind god, so
   "stern vs merciful" measured passive against kind: stern was less obedient than merciful (0.33 vs 0.45) and ended forgotten
   8 of 8.
   Fix in Tools/simrun/Policies.cpp: stern now plays the same kind god as merciful, with the same words (1 a year) and the same
   Awe reserve; only the judging habit differs. On seeds 1-16:
   - stern's obedience at 2,500 is >= merciful's on 14 of 16 seeds.
   - The +0.1 bar is met on 2 of 16. Too few judgements reach each people, the same reason judge-deeds vs judge-hearts fails.
   - stern now ends beloved 16 of 16 (target: feared or parting >= 5/8; still failing).
Tests adjusted for the new rules: test_cards.cpp, 2 tests. The maxOpen test drives 400 years instead of 1,000, so its 5,000-year
window is not capped mid-test. In the closed-cards save-bound test, the last open MessengerChoice is for a second people. No
expectation was weakened.

Found and reported, not changed
- Starvation by priority, which follows the spec. One people whose matters are always graver takes every card the budget allows:
  19 of 19 openings in 3,000 years, the four quieter peoples 0. The cultureGap (150 years) never binds while the budget refills
  more slowly (targetGap 170), and severity >= 0.8 jumps the gap anyway. Possible fixes: cultureGap >= targetGap, or a waiting
  bonus for a people with no card in N years. That is a lead / B2 decision; I tried a narrow guard and reverted it as too small.
- An Exact word naming a figure that does not exist (or another people's messenger) silently goes through the people's own
  messenger, or through the sky under Wonders, at the doubled Exact-from-the-sky cost (120 instead of 60). No bad id is stored.
  The chooser must show canSpeak's cost.
