WHAT CHANGED (all in Game/Planet/*, Scripts/planet*, Content/Planet/*)
1 Sea, far field (Scripts/planet/water.hlsl, Scripts/planet.py: new CameraVectorWS input, Emissive output on M_PlanetWater).
  Measured cause: from ~30 km up the engine's Single Layer Water gets neither sun in-scattering nor glint. With the atmosphere's
  ground lowered 2 km (-atmodrop=2) the open sea at orbit was the same grey-black, so it is not only the virtual-planet shadow;
  the navy came from the aerial perspective alone (known issue 1b). The water now lights itself past a ~25 m pixel footprint
  (Smooth01((fp-25)/220)): the sun's illuminance through the atmosphere transmittance LUT at the sea surface x a palette body
  colour (deep sapphire, cyan-teal shelves, turquoise first 12 m, darker lakes) x N.L, plus a GGX sun glint (roughness 0.07-0.22
  from the unresolved wave slopes; brighter in the wind lanes, sharper in slicks). Near the coast the engine's own SLW lighting
  (shallows, foam swash, surf bands, swell glint, river plumes) is unchanged.
  Also: richer deep / shelf scattering coefficients.
2 Planet card polygons: the water lift (first attempt, DmPlanet.cpp BuildPatch: water raised by its triangles' sag, max 7-29 m
  logged as "water lift max") plus the self-lit far sea: the card-framing world shot shows no straight-edged pale patches.
3 Molten world (Scripts/planet/land.hlsl): the uniform cell-edge crack net is gone. Rifts now follow warped ridge lines in
  3 km and 1 km sizes, only in about half of the crust (rift provinces), with a yellow hot core line and an orange halo;
  basins are a lava sea of dark skins torn by bright flow channels (ridges of a flow-warped field, 3 sizes, slow convection);
  the seams at the future shores stay the brightest; the heat haze spills wider over the crust and lifts it toward hot ochre.
4 Life wave (land.hlsl): a visible green front: lime-green glow with a gold-green core at the leading edge, fresh growth green
  under it, lighter mist (the first attempt's cream glow read as white blobs at 140 km).
5 Strategy palette (Game/Planet/DmPlanetMaps.cpp macro albedo, S2 stylised realism + strategy grade in land.hlsl): warm ochre
  sand and golden steppe (earth-toned, not mustard), cleaner leaf-green grass, deeper forest / jungle, blue-green taiga.
