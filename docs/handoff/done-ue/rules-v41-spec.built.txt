What it is
- The buildable spec for the next sim wave, "rules v4.1": Astra's T-006 bundle (11 findings) folded into gameplay-v4's
  Situations umbrella as one wave (the lead's PR 19 answer). Astra's numbers are tracking metrics until an owner
  30-minute session; construction invariants are ordinary SimTests.

Scope (section 1)
- All 11 findings are in, as rules R1-R11: weathering episodes; prayer choruses with once-per-chorus credit; Risks and
  pleas (Hunger, War, Plague, Sea, Fire "Tinder", the Ark race, plus the plea row) with stores and a slow war gate
  (vSlow, tau 20); Awe reserve bands (fading above 60 % after 20 idle years, x1.25 income below 15 %); Ark levers
  (memory at 25 % weight, sealed from 0.8; Inspire +0.02, wrath -setback/250 capped at 0.5, a Leave-taking +0.05);
  Unproven before maturity (year 250 plus a history); one attention ledger owned by the card director; climate-rescue
  pricing; Gifts (Monsoon or Ward, Star-fall or Guiding Light, 300 Awe); climate attribution and restoration episodes;
  a neutral score (Life + Continuity + Wonder).
- Rejected or deferred: preachers and Victory (Mythos owns them), eruption precursors (Mythos Omens), situation outcome
  memory effects (double counting), and callings, zones, setups, golden age, strangers and the nuclear ramp (later
  waves).

Key architecture decisions
- One attention budget: the director (Sim/Cards.*) keeps the ledger (Epochal > Crisis > Card > Notice; at most 4 loud
  entries per 10 s and 3 open crises). Situations report into it and never open cards. CardKind::Situation stays
  reserved, which amends SPEC-MYTHOS 0.3 and 2.2.
- New state World::rules (Sim/RulesTypes.h) in a new top-level save block "RULE" v1 (sub-blocks STOR VSLO SITU EPIS
  ARKL CLIM ATTN RNUM). Older builds skip it, BASE stays v1 and kVersion stays 2. simrun gets a second hash (hash2)
  that covers RULE.
- New module Sim/Situations.{h,cpp}: no rng, and it writes only its own list. The schedule line goes between
  mythos::yearly and cards::yearly, and powers::weather goes after cards::yearly.
- Every family is behind a TUNE flag that defaults off (the v3.1 / Mythos phase-0 guardrail holds 32/32).
  Balance.cpp turns the families on one at a time with tables; the Omen of Peace moves to Bronze with the slow gate.

Build plan (section 4)
- Gate: Mythos v1 merged (M7-M9, B1, V1). R0 contracts first, then six disjoint tracks (R1 situations, R2
  society-diplomacy, R3 economy-climate-life, R4 powers, R5 director-sim, R6 tools), then R7 chronicle, B41 balance and
  V41 independent verification. About 60 named SimTests with setups and expected values, 11 new scripted policies
  (responder, diplomat, coast-warden, guide-first, delay-first, monsoon, ward, star-fall, guiding-light, steward,
  accelerator), a simrun --rules report and a "rules" tracking table. The client is a later separate track mapped to
  SPEC-UI 4, 4b and 5.
