BLOCKER, not fixed (lead decision; planet water + sky). The builder's diagnosis is right but his remedy is not.
Lowering AtmoDropKm does expose the water, but the water itself is broken:
- With -atmodrop=0.5, shelf seas render pale grey at 400 km.
- With -atmodrop=2, almost the whole ocean renders pale grey. The cheap "never seen" deep floor (M_PlanetSeabed) shows
  through 150-500 m of water with its polygonal patch edges.
- A constant red water base colour at Opacity 1 renders navy even at -atmodrop=2. The Single Layer Water surface does not
  seem lit by the sun, and depth absorption barely acts.
- The builder's turbid green-teal shallows, silt cover and breakers therefore never show as designed. The drowned band
  reads as dark slate mud at 60 km. The depth gradient he showed is the land material's silt seabed seen through clear water.
- Only a sea fall at -atmodrop=0.5 shows a proper turquoise shelf grading to navy.
- The "nice navy ocean" of the shipped game is the atmosphere's ground, not water. This is probably also the integration
  report's "flat light-blue water".
Next step: someone owning both Game/Planet water and Game/Sky must find why SLW gets no sun and no depth extinction here.
Suspects: per-pixel atmosphere transmittance on water pixels at or below the bottom radius; SLW's flat-Z light-path
assumption on a sphere. Only then change AtmoDropKm.
