(1) The chronicle, in the approved voice (translated, wry, the god seen from below, no game terms). Every family has 3-8 variants, rotated least-used first.
- Alliances:
  - `alliance` formed: 5 lines, tellable notable.
  - Ended in peace: 4 lines, minor. Held back to the year's close.
  - A pair that falls out and makes up again within allianceGap (20 years) is told once.
  - Allies who come to blows: Diplomacy breaks the alliance just before war:declared in the same tick, so it is told as one betrayal (3 lines), never as "the alliance lapsed".
- Allies joining a war: war:declared.ally gets 4 lines naming the joiner, the enemy and the ally it defends ({c3}).
- Blame, two families:
  - "the god struck us" (blamed && !envied): 8 lines.
  - "the god favoured them" (envied): 7 lines.
  - A betrayal that was blamed takes a suffix of either kind.
- Payload: war:declared gains a fifth key, `envied` (Events.h/.cpp). It is true when the god's favour of b over a was at least blameMin and weighed more than its wrath on a. It is never true without blamed.
- No god in unblamed wars: the one plain war line that named the god ("saying the god was with them") lost that clause, and the clause moved to the thanked lines. Only blamed and thanked lines name the god. Approved voice line v27c ("both sides prayed to the same sky") was kept: it names prayer, not the god's hand.
- Thanks: war:ended.thanked gets 5 lines in which the victors thank their god for the victory and for the peace. One tells the beaten people's love growing cold, matching blameLove.
- The calming Omen (no war) now has its own 7 lines. It no longer reuses "laid down their spears". {c} is the people it was cast on, {c2} the neighbour it calmed. A suffix names any further calmed peoples ("The same calm came over the A and the B").
- An Omen that ends a war and also calms other neighbours writes the war line plus a suffix naming them.
- How it works: Chronicle records the Omen at power:used, which comes after the effect and before the witnesses. It names the target town, its people and the neighbours calmed: those sworn this very year (society::swornUntil, new and read-only) with no war ended between them this tick. It tells everything once, at the first witness.
- Tests (test_chronicle_diplomacy, run.ps1 chronicle and all):
  - 60 wars: 0 of 20 plain lines name the god, every struck and envied line does, and struck lines never use envy words.
  - Thanked victories name the god; unthanked ones do not.
  - Alliance formed, ended, betrayed and joined lines name the right peoples.
  - The calming Omen is one entry naming all 3 peoples, not "laid down their spears". The war Omen with a calmed neighbour is one entry, with no "draw" beside it.

(2) The Omen of Peace. TUNE defaults keep v3.1; Balance.cpp switches the first two on.
- omenWide: an Omen that ends a people's wars also calms every other tense neighbour of that people (society::tense: 40-year truce, v + 0.4, sworn). They witness it too.
- omenNoEnvy: the Omen leaves no envy. Its witnesses carry the new WitnessOpts.favour = false, so they add nothing to society_fgrace (before, every third people envied the two armies the god had just blessed). A pair under a sworn peace does not envy each other's favour while the oath lasts.
- omenAge = 1 (Bronze) was tested and left off (default -1 = the table's Iron). The sim-findings-2 numbers pointed to it (Bronze is 18 % at war), but in the run it made things worse:
  - kind's median war share rose 7.7 -> 11.1 %, and kind < cruel fell to 6/8.
  - It cost Bronze Awe, and the sworn early peoples fought more in Iron and Sail (kind wars a run, median 24 -> 36).
  - peacemaker fell to 1.2 % but missed first-war-by-1300 on 1/8.
  - Evidence: exp-wide-noenvy-bronze-seeds1-8.txt.
