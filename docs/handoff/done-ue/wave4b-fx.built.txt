- Volcano: dense charcoal ash column with buoyancy (rises ~8-10 km, spreads downwind), no incandescence on ash (that made the
  plume salmon-pink); lava fountain = flame jet + short incandescent bombs (no more 55 radial streaks/s = the "starburst");

  vent light radius 7 -> 3.5 km.
- Bloom / seed: one wave per held brush that follows and holds (was a new wave every 0.7 s = concentric target rings);

  rendered relief and was invisible), faint green wash, tiny pollen motes. Motes are capped at 3.5 px half size and the
  four-point star glint is opt-in (par > 0.5): no more screen-filling sparkles close up.
- Lightning: afterglow (violet-blue channel ghost ~1 s, >= 2.5 px wide so it reads at strategy distance), ember at the
  strike point, lights the real cloud deck (ADmSky::Strike), smaller sky flash.
- Meteor impact: scorched ejecta rays (44, linger 48 s, read from orbit), brief Wilson condensation dome, softer and wider
  shock front (was a crisp white UI circle), dust wall alpha down.
- Blessing (heal / inspire / consecrate / answered prayer): broad warm shaft from cloud-base height leaning toward the sun,
  crepuscular strands and dust motes inside (Beam shape par > 0), soft opening glow, warm pool + lingering light (was a
  7 km neon column with a ring). Prophet: soft rising column (the 8-ray lens-flare star is gone).
- Fire: ember bed under the flames (reads as a glowing front), darker smoke, day visibility floor 0.15 -> 0.4.
- Global: renderer depth-fade floor = 1.2 % of distance (ground quads with 30-60 m fades made golden per-pixel speckle under
  fires and strikes); incandescence fades out below ~900 K (dull red on grey smoke read pink); FX lights by day 0.2 -> 0.06,
  smoke underlight by day 0.25 -> 0.1; softer sunset tint on lit smoke.
- Scenarios: Godly() also raises the world age to Industry (v3 unlocks Volcano by age; fx-volcano was REFUSED on the Sail
  fixture and showed nothing). Not Atomic/Space (Monsoon / Star-fall would change rain and meteor).
