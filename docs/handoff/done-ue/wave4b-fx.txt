- Defects found in the builder's life wave (FGreenWave, Game/FX/DmFxEffects.cpp):
  1. Green blots ON THE SEA round islets and along coasts (ue-fx-seed-v4b-f260: three green discs on the water by the
     islet; ue-fx-bloom-v4b-f300: green haze over the bay west of the front). Cause: coast test = nearest sim cell
     (CellAt + IsSeaCell). A cell's Voronoi region straddles the drawn coastline by up to a cell (~1.7 km on the
     40962-cell planet), and the 4-probe "half size near coasts" still left a 0.56-cell patch on a one-cell islet.
  2. Seam ticks across the gold edge at every bend (ue-fx-bloom-4c-f300 / v4b-f300). Cause: 120 segments with a
     half-width of 0.035 x radius, i.e. every Bolt quad was WIDER than it was long, so camera-facing quads of
     neighbouring segments wedged at each bend.
  3. The edge read cream-white, not gold (1.45 additive over sunlit beige land tonemaps to white; Bolt par 200 adds a
     white core), and its ends at the coast were whole segments: stubs hanging over the water or stopping short.
  4. By night the lit, alpha-blended green tint is near black while exposure lifts the ground: the wave left a dark
     olive smudge (ue-fx-bloom-night-v4b3-f260, -sun=-10,61).
  5. (ADmFx::DrawParticles, Game/FX/DmFx.cpp) the builder's sub-pixel fix grows S, then a velocity streak's length is
     S x Aspect, so a widened streak also gets longer and keeps Grow x its energy, not 1 x as its comment says.
     TRIED and REVERTED: keeping the pre-grow length made every ember a bright sub-pixel-long dot, and the meteor's
     burning forest became a carpet of golden grain (shots/ue-fx-meteor-main-v4b-f300.png vs -v4b2-f300.png). The
     builder's longer, dimmer streak is what reads right; left as is, with a comment saying why.

Fixes (in the track's own files; synced to main)
- DmFx.h / DmFx.cpp: new ADmFx::IsLandAt(Dir): land at sub-cell precision, elevation above sea level interpolated
  over the sim triangle holding the direction (same gnomonic barycentrics as the picker), i.e. the drawn coast.
- FGreenWave edge: 72 segments (each ~2x as long as the line is wide), half-width 0.08 km + 0.02 x radius, deeper
  gold Rgb(1, 0.6, 0.1) x 1.7 with Bolt par 225 (no white core), plus a soft green-gold glow band (1.8x width,
  par 255, 0.4). Segments are trimmed to the shore by a 4-step bisection on IsLandAt, so the line ends on the beach;
  a trimmed segment keeps its own length so its end ramp fades out there. Top harmonic 23 -> 17 (3 samples per period
  at 72 segments read as jaggies).
- FGreenWave tint: patch centres need IsLandAt; each patch takes the largest size of {1, 0.6, 0.36} x its grid size
  whose 8 probes at 0.95 of its radius are all land, else it is dropped. Alpha x (0.3 + 0.7 x DayAt).
- DrawParticles: unchanged in the end (see 5; only a comment added).
