Summary. The builder's claims reproduce on 8 new seeds (301-308) almost everywhere, with one exception. "kind's peoples are at war less than cruel's on >= 5/6" held on only 5/8 new seeds with the builder's code. More generally, a kind god's world is at war about as often as a world the god ignores. I fixed one latent bug in Diplomacy and changed the scripted kind player so that an Omen against a war comes before its two prayers of the year; with that the target holds 7/8 on both seed sets (14/16). Only the four design-review targets that were already failing still fail.

Fixes (owned files only; Game/, Names.*, Chronicle.* untouched):
- Sim/Diplomacy.cpp (bug): the yearly loop over open wars ran on pairs whose war had just ended earlier in the same loop. makePeace ends a principal war together with the wars its allies joined. A joined pair later in the list still got war weariness, a second peace attempt and the 1.5 %/yr nuclear draw: a bomb between two peoples at peace. The loop now skips pairs no longer at war. On 16 seeds x 20 policies no table changed, so the case is rare, but the bug was real.
- Tools/simrun/Policies.cpp (kind player): one Omen of Peace on a warring people now comes first, before the year's two prayers. Before, a busy prayer year left the war running.
  - kind < cruel at war: 5/8 -> 7/8 on seeds 301-308, and 7/8 on seeds 1-8.
  - kind median at war 9.8 % (seeds 1-8) and 10.0 % (301-308); passive 9.9 / 9.8; cruel 15.9 / 17.2.
  - Every other kind target is unchanged: beloved 16/16, Ark >= 150 y before passive 15/16, thanked 15/16, Omens 33-35 a game, atomic-kind / late-kind not beloved 15/16.
- Tools/simrun: three adversarial players, also in --policy all:
  - peacemaker: casts only the Omen, up to 3 a year, on any people at war or on the brink.
  - favourite: blesses one people only.
  - scourge: strikes only the smallest people, never the last.
- SimTests/test_integration_findings2.cpp (new): on seeds 303 and 306 (L 6) it saves at the exact tick of the first war:declared and at the tick of the next world age. Every relation (war, since, truce, aggressor, v, trade) and every vars.num key (war tallies, alliances, oaths, feuds, favour) survives exactly. Two worlds loaded from the same bytes replay 300 years with the same hash.
- Tried and reverted (numbers in verify/exp-*): weakening envy.
  - envyK 0.001, envyK 0, envyRef 0.04, and a split grace / wrath envy reference.
  - They raised kind < cruel on seeds 301-308 but lowered it on seeds 1-8.

Trying to break it:
- War spirals: none.
  - Feuds lengthen truces: the chronicles show "the old enemies" fighting again after long truces, not every 40 years.
  - Weary or war-worn peoples stand down; stalled wars end in a draw.
  - The worst passive seed (301, 24.4 %) is at war 41 % of its Sail age and 23-30 % of the later ages. That is a sustained war theme, not constant war.
- Permanent peace: not reachable.
  - peacemaker casts 80-82 Omens a game and still sees 5.4-8.0 % of years at war (median; passive 9.8-9.9). The Omen wakes only in the Iron age, so Bronze-age wars go on.
  - It is not an Awe exploit to "beloved" either: 2/8 and 3/8 beloved, the rest parting.
- Extinction cascades: none. Cultures that died per run: usually 0-5 (passive 0-7), up to 10 only in runs that birthed 15. No policy except doom ever lost every people.
  - scourge (wrath on the smallest people only): 0.6-26.7 % at war, ends forgotten 7/8, no cascade. The scourged rarely have the 0.6 strength to attack.
- Exploiting the new rules: favouritism is the strongest lever.
  - favourite (bless one people) is at war 13-18 % (median), close to cruel's 16-17 %, through envy, and still ends beloved 8/8 and 6/8. This reads as intended ("a god's favourite is envied"); the lead should confirm.
  - A prayers-only god is "blamed" for 73-76 wars per 8 runs (cruel 113-131): blame counts envy of answered neighbours. The HUD and chronicle should say "envied" rather than "blamed the god" when the defender was the one favoured (the event has no field to tell the two apart; see the open items).
- Determinism:
  - --hash three times (seeds 301-306 x passive/kind/cruel/peacemaker x 3,000 y, --jobs 10/4/10): 24/24 identical hashes and identical chronicle entry counts.
  - simrun --saveload at seed 302's first war (443), Bronze (345), Iron (1046) and Industry (2673): each re-saves identically and replays to the same hash twice. Each ends ark/forgotten in 3,974-4,077 (uninterrupted 3,979).
- run.ps1 all: 762 passed, 0 failed.

The chronicle's shape (read seed 305 kind and seed 301 passive in full, 187-192 notable entries each):
- Clearly better. The first quarrel and war come at 385-641 and an early people is wiped out (452 / 710). Secessions come in the Iron age, then feuds with long memories ("War came between the Kosdeonoi and the Dolusloi, the old enemies"), conquests and tribute, and wars that "wore themselves out". A kind god's Omens punctuate the middle ages. The late ages turn to famine, warming seas, abandoned towns and a quieter end.
- The weak spots are the chronicle's, not the sim's:
  - The calming Omen (no war) reuses "laid down their spears".
  - Alliances, calls to arms, `blamed` and `thanked` have no lines, so the player never reads the new diplomacy.
  - Passive worlds still draw god-flavoured war lines ("saying the god was with them") although blamed = 0. Those lines should key on the new `blamed` field.
