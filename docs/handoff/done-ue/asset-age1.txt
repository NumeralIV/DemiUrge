summary:
  Rebuilt the kit from scratch (build_age1.py --textures). The 22 textures and manifest.json came out byte-identical
  to the builder's, so the build is reproducible. Re-imported all 56 FBX and checked them against docs/ASSETS.md:
  - Z is up and the front faces -Y.
  - Pivots are at the footprint centre at Z=0, and every building reaches down to -0.3 m.
  - LodGroup is present, with LOD0-2 and LOD3 hulls over 10 m.
  - Each mesh has at most 3 slots, in the order Structure/Roof/Detail.
  - Naming follows SM_/M_/MI_/T_.
  - Every mesh has one UV map (tiling and trim, no baked maps) and the Col attribute.
  - Smoothing, tangents and binormals are in the FBX.
  - Closed parts face outward.
  - The manifest lists footprint, height, tris per LOD, slots, textures, sockets, licence, script, states, cultures
    and sim_mapping.
  Scale reads correctly against the 1.8 m figure (doors 1.8 m, granary drums 4.1 m across, palisade ~4 m, temple 30 m
  base and 14 m tall). All renders went through tools/render_slot.py.

art direction vs concept (age-1-bronze, building-granary, building-temple):
  + At 300 m and 1 km the village reads like the concept:
    - the oval palisade on an earth bank is the strongest line;
    - the 3-tier stepped temple is the centre anchor;
    - the granary is a tight cluster of round thatched drums on a dry-stone platform with golden heaps;
    - the fields are hard-edged golden and green rectangles;
    - the jetty has reed boats.
  + The four house types read distinctly from above (gable, hip with porch, flat clay with roof room, yard house), and
    the damage states are legible.
  - At 40 m it is clean mid-fidelity game art, not near-photoreal:
    - the procedural textures are tidy;
    - walls and thatch share one saturated orange-brown, so the concept's grey weathered timber and greyer straw do
      not separate;
    - the temple is plain blocky mudbrick, with no batter and a barely visible painted frieze.
  - The civic meshes are under the 8-20k range: granary 7.1k, temple 3.4k, court 1.0k. This is fine for performance
    but leaves the hero temple thin at 40 m.

open (not fixed, for the lead):
  - Thatch slabs and wall columns leave coincident internal faces, so some meshes have non-manifold edges. Examples:
    House B LOD0 82, Gate 108, Temple 34. They render fine, but they are not clean "closed parts".
  - The trim sheet packs a low-roughness water strip between the grain and hide strips, so at far mips grain heaps
    may catch a little gloss.
  - The landing's pivot is ~0.9 m off its footprint centre in Y (waterline under the jetty, documented).
  - Construction and Damaged clutter overhangs some footprints. Fallen logs reach ~1.3 m past the palisade segment
    end; temple construction rubble reaches 4.7 m past the base.
  - Temple Ruin LOD0 shows thin bright slivers on its eroded mound.
  - Nothing has been imported into Unreal yet.
