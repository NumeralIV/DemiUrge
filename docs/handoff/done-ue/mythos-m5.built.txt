Built
- Mythos is read only at MythosTune::phase >= 1 (at phase 0 Society / Diplomacy do not even call it: the v3.1 call
  graph, cost and bits are kept). Multipliers of 1 are exact, offsets are added only when non-zero, non-finite
  records count as neutral, and nothing new draws on w.rng (forks only).
- Factors reads. Society: devotionBase (baseline), loveTarget (love drifts toward it), prayerChance x expect[kind]
  (above 1 the devotion gate is prayDevotion / p, below 1 a roll forked "myth:society:pray:<sid>:<year>", 0 = no
  prayer), unansweredPenalty, answeredGain (an answered prayer's faith and its progress-year), aweS (the culture's
  weight in S), schism (estrangement years needed / schism, 0 = no secession); archetypeParams.aweIncome multiplies
  the income. Diplomacy: aggression (added to the trait), warV (the aggressor's, added to the -0.5 gate),
  warStrength (the aggressor's, x the 0.6 gate), relDrift (each side's, added for a pair in contact), battle
  (attacker), defence (defender), harmTaken (defender culture x townFactors: capture needs strength / harmTaken),
  plagueHop (the RECEIVING culture's), tradeEvery (the town's culture).
- witnessScale (3.2) in witness: faith (also the answered-prayer faith), jaded, long memory, favour sums; natural
  events are never scaled; god.mercy / wrath and the brush love scale use the unscaled act.
- New traits (3.5): compassion / ambition / courage rolled at phase >= 1 from the fork "myth:society:traits:<id>"
  (0.5 at phase 0: extensionsDefault holds), daughters drift. shapeTraits sets the five shaped traits (clamped 0..1,
  refuses a bad / dead culture or a NaN) and records them (vars "society_shape_<trait>"); later ROOT peoples inherit
  the shaping +- traitDrift.
- The fear rule by aspect (3.3): a Threat people gets the v3 fear term whatever its love, and courage softens fear's
  bonus x (1 - 0.5 courage) (3.5); both only where mythos acts on the culture: phase >= 1 and (emergent, or its
  Factors are not neutral).
- Victory and Sun prayers (3.10). Victory: a capital at war with exhaustion < victoryExhaustion; it lasts while the
  war does; answered (in witness, wherever the act falls) by any non-natural harm of the god on a people it is at war
  with. Sun: territory wet > sunWet for sunYears years (vars "society_wet_<sid>"), a storm over it, or a flooded
  territory (mean wet >= 0.95); it lasts while wet > sunWet, storm or flood; answered by Raise Land on a flooded or
  sodden town, or through answerPrayer.
- Messengers (3.8): visits raise devotion by prophetDevotion x (0.5 + credibility) (unnamed prophet = x 1); a Child
  lives CharactersTune::childYears, a Shepherd travels every shepherdTravel years.
- Owner APIs: nudge (clamped, NaN ignored), answerPrayer(sid, scale) (the answered effects at scale <= 1: devotion,
  love, memory, blessed x scale, answered count, prayer:answered, progress-year x scale), remember (grace / wrath into
  the long memory and favour sums, heard; the Ark rule kept). secede stays neutral (M5b).

Deviations and decisions, LOUDLY
1. Victory and Sun start only with phase >= 1 AND MythosTune::emergent: they move a passive world (unanswered
   penalties, the prayer slot, the memory's prayer counts), which SPEC-MYTHOS 5.1 rule 2 forbids with emergent off.
   So with emergent off no CompetingPrayers pair can form (M2 / B1: this is a "prayers" family for the balance
   tables). sunYears <= 0 or victoryExhaustion <= 0 switch either kind off on its own.
2. The Threat fear rule and the courage term are gated the same way (phase >= 1 and emergent or non-neutral Factors),
   because mythos::aspect carries no "effects apply" flag.
3. Constants without a TUNE field (headers frozen): courage's 0.5 on the fear bonus, the flood wetness 0.95, and
   answerPrayer's faith 0.1 are constexpr in Society.cpp.
4. "The god not raining there for 10 years after the card" (Sun) is not tracked by Society: the CompetingPrayers
   handler (M2) should call society::answerPrayer when it decides the Sun side is answered.
5. answerPrayer (planned for M5c) and remember (M5b) are implemented now (small, and the v1 competing-prayer middle
   way needs answerPrayer). The ShapePeople budget rule (two High at most, each High with a Low) is the card
   handler's (M1): shapeTraits takes values and only clamps.
6. Archetype aweIncome is applied by Society only at phase >= 1; M1 must not fold it into Factors::aweS too.
   harmTaken in battles is applied to capture strength only (not to the 15 % loss), so Civ may own losses.
7. The shared build dirs (SimTests/build/all, Tools/simrun/build) are used by several workflows at once; runs were
   retried on "Permission denied" / LNK1104 collisions.
