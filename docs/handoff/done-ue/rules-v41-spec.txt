WHAT I CHANGED (by severity)

Blocking defects fixed
1. Save and hash model was wrong. The draft said sim::fingerprint "cannot see RULE" and added a second hash (hash2).
   In fact fingerprint hashes the full v2 save whenever extensionsDefault is false, which is the case as soon as
   Mythos phase >= 1 writes any state. An always-written RULE block would therefore have broken the recorded phase-B1 hashes, and
   "SituationsTune::on changes neither hash" was false.
   Now: RULE is written only when the new state::rulesDefault is false, so a world with every flag off saves byte for
   byte as before. extensionsDefault also requires rulesDefault. Read-only proofs use simrun's existing `core` hash
   plus the rng state. hash2 is removed everywhere (2.3, 2.7, T1, T6, B41 step 1).
2. Flags-off writes would have broken the guardrail. civ_co2y_<id>, climate_uptake and powers_lastSpent went into
   vars.num (the v1 payload), and the climate ring, stores fill-on-rebuild and vSlow initialisation had no gate.
   cards_lastAnswer went into mythos.num and had no reader.
   Now: D5 says a family with its flag off writes nothing anywhere. The new gate ClimateTune::attribution covers the
   ring, the keys and attribution. The three scalars moved to rules.num. Stores and vSlow fill only with their flags
   on. cards_lastAnswer is deleted.
3. The schedule did not match the code. yearlyGroup runs mythos::yearly and cards::yearly inside one
   `if (mythosOn)`, so "situations::yearly runs at every phase" needed that block split. 2.4 now says so explicitly,
   and the yearly-order test checks phase 0 and phase 1. simrun must attach the new modules when it turns flags on
   after create (T6).
4. The Ark wrath push sat inside civ::strikeKnowledge, which lives in Economy.cpp, not Civ.cpp, and is also called
   by Doctrine's burning decree. That would have blamed the god for the people's own act.
   Now: the push sits in Powers' setBack and in one call at a god's meteor impact in Disasters.cpp (T4 owns that one
   line). strikeKnowledge is unchanged. The test moved from T3 to T4, with a Doctrine-burning counter-test.
5. Chorus counting was inconsistent. Only per-town answers were moved to the chorus; per-town prayer beginnings
   still counted (Society.cpp line 526). The Beloved-by-answered-prayers test would then have compared per-town
   beginnings with per-chorus credit, which harms the kind god. Now both move to the chorus (Victory and Sun keep
   per-town counting). The int truncation in memory() is documented. The T2 test checks both counts.
6. The rule could not meet its own target (metric 10). Decay "0.004 x aweMax a year" while income is still added
   cannot move passive off the cap while aweMax is below about 700: income is about 2.2-2.8 a year, decay 1.2-2.4.
   Now: while Fading, no income is added and the decay is net. The arithmetic is in the rule, Q3 is reworded, and
   the test setup includes income. This is the one place I changed a design number's meaning; see risk 1 below.

Double-counted attention fixed (R3, R7)
7. A War entering Crisis plus its own war:declared would have made two ledger entries. A risk row that a Mythos
   Warning card already announced would interrupt a second time. Added "one matter, one interruption": fold into the
   same-year Epochal entry, and record an omen-foreseen Rising as quiet. Each has a test.
8. CompetingPrayers pairs prayers in cards::yearly, after situations::yearly, so one town could sit in a plea and on
   a card at once. Added the handoff: the next pass removes the member and subtracts its peak, and the card owns the
   prayer. Tested.
9. The ledger kept "the last 64", so a busy Industry window could push loud entries out and miscount room. Quiet
   entries now drop first, and the window is defined as (year - 20, year].
10. Card openings had to wait for room with no bound, so the director could starve. Added attnHold 60: after 60 years
   of waiting the card opens quiet. This is a reviewer number and is marked as such. Noted that Epochal entries and
   big Crises can exceed "4 loud per 10 s" by design (metric 18).
11. "The first tsunami:hit of a wave" is not detectable (the event has no wave id). It is now tsunami:start.

Determinism and save gaps fixed
12. The War Watch ("below its value 5 years ago") and slope10 needed a per-pair vSlow history that is never saved.
    Both are now derived from state: falling means v < vSlow, and slope = (v - vSlow) / slowTau. The ETA formula
    and tests are updated.
13. Plea `affected` needed per-member peaks that were not stored. Added Situation::peak[8].
14. rescued[] had no "never" default; it is now -1e9. deserialize now also checks the lengths of index-keyed
    vectors, with a test.
15. The episode commit compared accumulated float dt. It now uses `held + 1e-9 >= 0.4`.
16. The episode witness would have gone through Society's brush pacing (miracle:witnessed only every 2nd call), so
    the "1 miracle:witnessed" test would fail on the first commit. Added WitnessOpts::episode (new), implemented
    by T2.
17. Situations now iterates disasters, omens and cards in id order, and uses Economy's BFS plague rings.

Untestable or wrong acceptance criteria fixed
18. "--saveload replays to the same hash2" is impossible: quantized fields differ after a load, as simrun already
    reports. It is now "the loaded world re-saves byte for byte, and two runs with the same saveload point end equal".
19. The rescue test was self-contradictory: a town 0.05 above the 100-year sea is not at Rising. Replaced with a
    consistent setup (margin(30) -0.02, gain 0.45 to safety).
20. The Notice test's 100-110 window was ambiguous. It now uses exact years and the window definition.
21. The 0.15 ms timing assert in SimTests (build type unknown) is now reported in SimTests and measured in simrun
    Release.
22. "Urgent pleas" (metric 2) is now defined as severity >= 0.25.
23. "The recorded 300-year trace (M5's)" now names test_diplomacy_v4_trace.cpp.
24. The absolute 30 ms tick gate already fails in the evidence: one run of 136 has a thread-CPU maximum of
    57.9 ms; the other 135 are 6.1-29.0 ms. The v4.1 cost gate is now relative: mean +0.25 ms, and each run's maximum
    no more than 10 % higher.

Ambiguity, names and sources
25. "R0-R7" meant both rules and tracks. For example, "Powers.cpp (R4)" means track 4, but R4 is the Awe-reserve
    rule. Tracks are renamed T0-T7 throughout; rules stay R1-R11, with a note at the top.
26. T0 now lists what is new: every section 3 symbol is new except a named list of existing ones, all checked in the
    code. hoardIdle, Memory::mature/choruses, every new TUNE field and WitnessOpts::episode are called out.
    "LifeTune dry" does not exist; it is now Life's ignition term (dryA, dryB).
27. Added 5.1, "Where the starting numbers come from": Astra, gameplay-v4, today's code, or this spec's own
    untested choices.
28. omenAge = 1: Balance.cpp records that it was tried and left off. Kind's war share rose 7.7 -> 11.1 % and kind
    < cruel fell 7/8 -> 6/8. B41 step 3, the gate table and owner Q4 now make it conditional on the slow gate
    restoring that gate, with a fallback.
29. A War or Ark answer on a capital was not detected as "answered", because War has no member towns. Detection
    now counts only the row's own answer powers, so an unrelated Rain does not count.
30. Smaller fixes: Disasters/SPEC-MYTHOS ownership rows; T0 owns its test_save and test_core_events cases and a
    one-line amendment note in SPEC-MYTHOS 0.3 and 2.2. Stores delay famine rain prayers (noted for metric 4).
    The Quiet and Ash multipliers also go under the neutral score. A Leave-taking costs the kind god one Wonder
    score line (noted). Gifts key on world age.

Checked and found sound (no change): the costs and setbacks against Powers.h and Balance (25/56.25/50/12.5,

REMAINING RISKS
1. Owner-facing design change: withholding income while Fading (point 6) is my reading of Astra's "decays until it
   reaches 60 %". It makes idleness costlier than the draft. Q3 asks about it, but the owner may prefer Astra's
   literal wording with a larger decay.
2. Many catalogue thresholds are this spec's untested choices (5.1, last row). The warning share (metric 6) and
   false-warning share (metric 7) depend on them, and B41 will likely retune several.
3. The slow gate plus omenAge may leave Bronze wars as unlisted notices if B41 step 3 fails. War rows would then
   start in Iron.
4. attnHold 60 and the quiet-first trimming are reviewer additions with no playtest behind them. The 15-28 cards
   target must be re-measured.
5. Gate: Mythos M7-M9, B1 and V1 have no markers, yet M7/M9 code is already present in Sim.cpp, Powers.h and
   simrun, so other workflows are still active in these files (Chronicle.cpp changed today). Phase-B1 hashes must
   exist before T0 can prove its guardrail at phase 1.
6. The 24 KB RULE size is an estimate (stores and rescued grow with every settlement ever founded). V41 measures
   it.
7. The hoarder and prayers policies give identical numbers in sim-findings-2 (the author's note), so the "builder
   vs hoarder" metric (13) rests on weak evidence.
8. I spot-checked the evidence numbers (strokes, Beloved before year 20, aweMax, CPU maxima, the Omen trial) but
   did not re-derive every section 5 "now" cell.
