Fixes (owned files only; Game/, Names.*, Tools/simrun untouched):
- Sim/Chronicle.cpp (bug, systematic): the duplicate Omen line.
  - Cause: when the Omen answers a people's peace prayer, Society emits prayer:answered inside the witness, before miracle:witnessed. The answer ("The Itindakani prayed for peace, and Yelondo gave it.") consumed the war it ended, so tellOmen found no enemy and fell back to a second, enemy-less line ("An omen of peace was seen over Ingawo, and the Itindakani laid down their spears.").
  - Fix: the chronicle now notes that a peace prayer was answered this tick (ChronicleScratch.peaceAns*). In that case the bare fallback line is not written. The Omen's war and calm lines are unchanged.
  - Effect on 601-606 x 3,000 y: exactly one duplicate entry removed in each of the 8 kind/peacemaker runs; none in passive or cruel.
- Sim/Chronicle.cpp (bug, latent): the allies' wars an Omen ended.
  - Cause: Diplomacy's makePeace also ends the wars allies joined in the war the Omen ended. tellOmen only took the pending draws involving the people the Omen was cast on, so an ally's war was told one tick later as a draw ("ended because both had run out of sons").
  - Fix: those draws are now taken into the Omen entry with a new suffix family that names the allies, 2 lines plus 2 for when calmed neighbours are named too. Example: "The allies of the Umari, the Vaeli, went home with them."
  - I did not see this in the four full chronicles I read: joined wars are rare under kind, 0-10 a run.
- Sim/Chronicle.cpp (style): the "ring of light" calming line is shorter. With a suffix it ran to about 50 words; it is now about 40, inside the guide's 45.
- Prose only: none of the fixes touches the sim stream. On 601-606 every per-seed row, event count and the diplomacy table are identical before and after; only the chronicle's own count and the fingerprint (which includes the chronicle text) differ.

Trying to abuse the stronger Omen. The abuse players are in a scratch copy of simrun; the diff is in verify/abuse-policies.diff.txt. Tools/simrun itself is unchanged.
- Omen spam, no limit per year (Awe-limited): 78-92 Omens a game, war 14.0 % median, no better than peacemaker (12.0) or kind (12.4). Awe is the real limit.
- Omen spam with unlimited Awe (Awe refilled before every cast):
  - 96-276 Omens a game, war 1.6 / 3.7 / 5.7 %. Wars after Bronze fall to about 0.2-0.3 an age; the Bronze wars stay.
  - Nothing breaks: no NaN, Ark 6/6, chronicles of normal size (1,085-1,378 entries). It is not a beloved exploit either: beloved 4/6, parting 2/6.
  - So permanent peace needs unlimited Awe; with real Awe it cannot be reached.
- The favourite shielded by Omens (bless one people and cast the Omen whenever it is at war or on the brink, so that its oaths stop its neighbours' envy): war went up, from 12.4 to 19.8 % median (wars 34 -> 41). Envy still comes from unsworn neighbours, and the Awe spent on the Omens is not spent on blessings. It ends beloved 6/6, as without the shield. Not an exploit.
- omenNoEnvy keeps the Omen's grace in the long memory (Ark verdict) and leaves it out only of the fading favour that drives envy. That is intended, but it makes the Omen a love source with no envy cost. The unlimited-Awe run shows it is still not a beloved shortcut.
