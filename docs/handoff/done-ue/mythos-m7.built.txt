Built (Sim/Powers.cpp, Sim/Disasters.cpp, SimTests/test_powers_v4.cpp, SimTests/test_powers_v4_disasters.cpp)
- Phase gating in phaseLock: a row with unlock.mythos > MythosTune::phase is Locked. At phase 0 every Word power is
  Locked, shows its table price and never calls a mythos module.
  - speak needs phase 1 and the Stone age.
  - bless needs phase 2 and Bronze; test needs phase 2 and Iron.
  - reveal needs phase 3 and Bronze; renew needs phase 3 and Iron.
  - aweMax never unlocks a Word power.
- speak takes UseOpts.word:
  - can() asks doctrine::canSpeak with a probe Dream Answer. The 10-year gap gives Target / why None; Doctrine's Target
    gives why Settlement.
  - cost() shows the cheapest word for the clicked town's people, from doctrine::wordCost.
  - use() checks canSpeak with the chosen word and calls doctrine::speak, and only then pays k.cost. It then emits
    power:used and counts the deed. Powers does not witness the word.
  - The one change this attempt made: a Dream with no town named (word.ref -1) is dreamed in the clicked town.
- bless, test, reveal and renew go to their owners: mythos::bless, characters::test, doctrine::reveal and
  renewal::begin.
  - Prices: bless by kind (MythosTune), test by trial (CharactersTune::trialCost), reveal by knowledge
    (DoctrineTune::revealCost). renew needs RenewalTune::minAwe and costs all the Awe the god has.
  - Each is paid only after the owner accepts. Nothing is spent on a refused word, and no power:used is emitted.
  - When the god cannot pay: awe:short with the power's id, and the owner is not asked.
  - bless is witnessed with the table's Radius. test needs a living ruler, messenger, healer or hero at the town.
  - renew happens once per world (mythos.renewal or renewedYear set means Target).
- powers::pay(what, amount):
  - Free when amount <= 0. A non-finite amount is refused with no event.
  - When short of Awe: false and awe:short { what }, at most once per aweShortEvery per act, on the Powers clock.
  - Never a deed, never a power:used event.
- The Messenger choice (3.8): prophet with UseOpts::choose at phase >= 1 opens the MessengerChoice card through
  cards::open.
  - Not budgeted. ref[0] is the town; data holds armed = 1 and decade.
  - Nothing is spent and nothing is witnessed. The card's answer pays the prophet price (Characters' choiceCost).
  - Refused when the culture has a prophet or a messenger, or has no candidates.
  - choose false (the default), or phase 0, gives the v3.1 prophet. The client sets choose when arming.
- Withdrawal (3.29): at phase >= 3, while mythos.withdrawnUntil > year, every power refuses with Reason::Withdrawn.
  This is a v3 item, harmless before phase 3.
- Disasters, natural():
  - Expected counts are multiplied by the archetype. Every type gets x natural; quakes and eruptions also get
    x quakeVolcano; droughts also get x drought.
  - Balanced is x 1.0 exactly, so the doubles and draws are bit-identical.
  - Counts over 3,000 years are within 10 % of the table: Generous x 0.6; Dangerous lightning x 1.6, droughts x 2.08,
    quakes x 3.2.
- Factors::naturalRate is read only at phase >= 1, and only when some live culture's rate is not 1. Counts are
  multiplied by the largest rate. An event aimed at a culture's land is kept with probability rate / largest, from a
  w.rng fork, so the main stream does not move. Rate 0 means never struck; unowned land counts as rate 1.
  Mythos currently leaves naturalRate at 1 and gives the archetype directly; there is no double count.
- Strange storm ring (3.1): one storm with data["ring"] = 1.
  - Placement: over the sea cell farthest in hops from land (cached in vars.num "disasters_ring_cell" and
    "disasters_ring_hops").
  - Radius: 0.8 x that distance, between 0.08 and 0.25. No land lies under it.
  - Behaviour: power 0.9, lives 150 years, does not drift or weaken, and draws nothing while it lives. Forming it calls
    start(Storm) once, which draws once. natural() re-forms it the next year, even at calamity 0.
  - It is visible to the client as an ordinary storm disaster with the "ring" flag.
