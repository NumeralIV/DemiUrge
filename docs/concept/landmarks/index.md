# T-002 — Landmarks, wonders and god powers (concept art, Grok)

Visual targets for buildings, wonders and the god's powers. All twenty images are original: they
were generated with Grok Bot's image generator from the prompts below, written for this task. No
existing game, film or named artist was referenced or imitated. Each image is 1280 × 720 (16:9) PNG.

**Buildings and wonders:** same camera rules as T-001 (high oblique, about 40° down, roughly
300 m away so a single building fills the frame; golden hour, sun low on the left). Each building is
shown in the age in which `SPEC-SIM` §3.7 first allows it, so it can sit beside the matching T-001
age image.

**Powers and disasters:** seen from 3–20 km up, as the player-god sees them, with the brief that
effects must stay readable at a distance through clear shapes, light and colour rather than noise.
