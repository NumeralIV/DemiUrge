# T-001 — Settlements through the ages (concept art, Grok)

Visual targets for close-up settlements. All eleven images are original: they were generated with
Grok Bot's image generator from the prompts below, written for this task. No existing game, film or
named artist was referenced or imitated. Each image is 1280 × 720 (16:9) PNG.

**How the set was made.** `age-0-stone.png` was generated first. Each later age was generated with
the previous age's image as a reference ("same river valley, same camera and light"), so the seven
ages show **one place growing** from a hide-hut camp into a spaceport capital: the same river bend,
the same distant hills, the same low sun from the left, and a central temple that appears in the
Bronze age and is kept in the old core through every later age. `city-night.png` was generated with
`age-5-atomic.png` as a reference, so it is the same city at night. The three cultures were
generated without references.

**Camera for all images:** high oblique, roughly 40–45° down, 300 m–1 km from the settlement
(closer for camps, further for metropolises), golden hour with the sun low on the left.

`age-progression.jpg` is a contact sheet of the seven ages side by side (Stone, Bronze, Iron, Sail
on the top row; Industry, Atomic, Space below).

## age-0-stone.png — Stone age camp

A seasonal camp of about fifteen domed hide-and-thatch huts in a clearing by a river bend: central
fire pit with a single smoke column, drying racks, worn footpaths, no fields and no walls. Wild
forest presses in on every side.

**Prompt:**
> Stone Age camp: a small seasonal camp of about fifteen round huts made of bent wooden poles covered in hides and thatch, a central fire pit with thin smoke, drying racks with hides, a ring of stones, worn footpaths in the grass, a few tiny figures, no fields, no walls, beside a clear river bend at the edge of a wild oak and pine forest. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 600 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole settlement in the centre of frame and readable from above with clear roof shapes and paths, surrounding green river valley, rolling hills and distant hazy mountains, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

**For the 3D artist:**
- Huts are low domes/cones about 4–5 m across, scattered loosely in a rough ring around one open
  hearth; no straight lines anywhere in the layout.
- Materials: brown thatch and hide with visible ribbing, bare trampled earth paths radiating from
  the fire. Palette is warm straw and brown against saturated grass green.
- The fire and its smoke column are the one focal point; at game distance the camp reads as "a ring
  of dots + a smoke plume".
- The forest edge is the boundary: the camp is a clearing, not a cleared landscape.

Same valley. About forty rectangular timber and mud-brick houses with thatch inside an oval timber
palisade, a small stepped mud-brick shrine in the middle, beehive granaries, a few rectangular

> Bronze Age village in the same river valley, same camera and light as the reference image: about forty rectangular mud-brick and timber houses with thatched and flat clay roofs clustered on a low rise, a small stepped mud-brick temple platform at the centre, round clay granaries, a low earthen bank with a wooden palisade around the core, patchwork of small irregular wheat and barley fields and an orchard outside the bank, dirt tracks, a wooden jetty and reed boats on the river, cattle pens, thin hearth smoke. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 600 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole settlement in the centre of frame and readable from above with clear roofs, lanes, walls and fields, surrounding green river valley, forest edges and distant hazy mountains, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-0-stone.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist:**
- First hard edges: rectangular houses with ridged thatch roofs, an oval vertical-log palisade with
  a gate toward the river. The palisade is the strongest silhouette from above.
- The stepped mud-brick shrine (3 tiers, square) sits at the exact centre: this is the `temple`
  building's ancestor and the eye's anchor.
- Fields are few, small and golden (ripe grain) with hard rectangular edges, contrasting with the
  wild green around them.
- Materials: ochre mud-brick, grey weathered timber, straw thatch; small round granaries as domes.

## age-2-iron.png — Iron age walled town

Same valley. The village has become a town of a couple of hundred stone and timber houses inside a
round grey stone curtain wall with square towers and a gatehouse; a pillared stone temple on a
plinth in the centre; forge smoke; a timber bridge and boats on the river; a patchwork of hedged
fields outside; a large open-cast mine on the far hillside.

**Prompt:**
> Iron Age walled town in the same river valley, same camera and light as the reference image, the settlement has grown: about two hundred stone and timber houses with steep thatch and dark wooden-shingle roofs packed along curving cobbled streets, a ring of grey stone walls with square towers and a gatehouse, an open market square with stalls at the centre, a stone temple with a pillared porch on the highest point, granaries, a smithy quarter with forge smoke, a stone bridge over the river with a small wooden port and moored boats, a larger patchwork of hedged fields and pastures outside the walls, forest pushed back to the hills, an open-cast mine scar on a hillside. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 700 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole town in the centre of frame and readable from above with clear roofs, streets, walls and fields, distant hazy mountains, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-1-bronze.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist:**
- The `walls` building: a continuous grey stone ring with regularly spaced square towers; it should
  be the clearest shape at game distance, with the dense brown roofscape inside it.
- The `temple` becomes a pillared rectangular hall with a pale gable roof, lighter than every roof
  around it so it pops from above.
- The `mine`: a terraced, ochre open-cast scar on a hillside, visible from a long way off.
- Fields now cover most of the valley floor in a quilt of green and gold, edged with hedgerows.

Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 800 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole city in the centre of frame and readable from above with clear roofs, streets, walls and harbour, distant hazy mountains, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no flags with writing, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-2-iron.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist:**
- Roof colour shifts from brown thatch to warm terracotta tile: the single biggest "new age" cue
  from above. Houses become 2–4 storey blocks with courtyards.
- The temple gains a lead-grey dome and a columned portico; the dome is the landmark from now on and
  is kept in every later age.
- The `port`: long stone quays and timber piers with large three-masted ships whose cream sails read
  as bright triangles on the water.
- Note: the generator kept the round wall from the Iron age rather than star-shaped bastions; for
  the 3D target, add angular bastions to separate the Sail-age walls from the Iron-age ring.

## age-4-industry.png — Industry age city

Same city. Brick terraces and slate roofs have spread far beyond the old wall (kept as a fragment);
long brick mills, tall chimneys, two gasometers, a big glass-and-iron train shed, rail lines and an
iron truss bridge, steamships with funnels at the docks.

**Prompt:**
> Industrial Age city on the same river and bay, same camera and light as the reference image, the city has burst out of its old walls (only fragments of the old wall and the domed temple remain in the old core): grids of red-brick terraced rows and dark slate roofs spreading across the valley, a factory district of long saw-tooth-roofed brick mills with tall smoking chimneys, gasometers, a railway with a steam train crossing an iron truss bridge, a rail yard and a station with a glass-and-iron roof, coal heaps, steamships with funnels and some old sailing ships at stone docks with cranes, a broad tree-lined boulevard and a park, fields pushed far out, a light brown smoke haze drifting across the scene. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 900 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole city readable from above with clear roofs, streets, rail lines and docks, distant hazy hills, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no signs, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-3-sail.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist:**
- Palette darkens and desaturates: red-brown brick, dark slate, soot. Greenery almost disappears
  from the city; this is the visible cost of industry.
- The `factory`: long rectangular brick sheds with tall round chimneys trailing smoke; chimneys are
  the vertical accents that read at any distance.
- Rails are the new linear element: straight parallel lines, a large curved glass station roof, and
  a dark iron truss bridge.
- In-game the city should feel hazier than earlier ages (smoke particles, slightly brown fog).

Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows across the towers, high oblique aerial view from about 1 kilometre away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole city readable from above with clear roofs, street grid, highways and port, distant hazy hills, rich natural colours, soft atmospheric haze, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no signs, no logos, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-4-industry.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist:**
- Verticality arrives: flat-roofed grey and beige towers 10–30 storeys tall, casting long shadows
  that read the scale at golden hour.
- Highways are the new dominant line: wide pale ribbons that sweep around the city, with long
  concrete bridges.
- The `plant` building: twin hyperbolic cooling towers with white steam plumes, placed apart from
  the city across the water; they are the signature landmark of this age.
- Street trees return in rows; suburbs on the hills are a fine texture of small roofs.

## culture-a.png — Cliff-Terrace people (invented culture, Iron age)

A town stacked up the face of a red sandstone canyon above a green river: cube houses of ochre and
rose plaster with small domed roofs, each roof the terrace of the house above, joined by switchback
stairs; deep-blue doors; cisterns and water channels; palm-like trees on every level; a huge arched
shrine niche carved into the rock at the top; a rope bridge across the canyon; terraced fields on
the river floor.

**Prompt:**
> Concept art of an invented Iron Age culture town (not based on any real-world culture): the Cliff-Terrace people, who build stepped towns up the side of a red sandstone canyon above a green river. Houses are stacked cubes of smooth ochre and rose plaster with low round domed roofs, each roof doubling as the terrace of the house above, linked by switchback stairs and narrow ledges; deep-blue painted doorways; round stone cisterns and water channels cascading down the terraces; hanging gardens and date-palm-like fan trees on every level; a great carved rock-face shrine with a tall arched niche at the top; rope-and-plank bridges spanning to the opposite cliff; terraced green fields along the river floor below. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 600 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole town readable from above with clear roofs, stairs and fields, soft atmospheric haze in the canyon, rich natural colours, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

**For the 3D artist:**
- Modular kit: a plaster cube (2–3 sizes) with a shallow dome on top; stacks in steps so every
  roof is visible from above. The domes give a "field of bubbles" read at distance.
- Palette: rose-ochre plaster on red rock, accented by deep cobalt doors and bright green palms.
  The town is tone-on-tone with the cliff; the greenery and blue are what separate it.
- Signature landmark: a tall arched niche carved into the cliff at the top (their shrine/temple).
- Prefers steep slopes and canyon sides; water runs visibly down the town in channels.

## culture-b.png — Lagoon-Weaver people (invented culture, Sail age)

A town on timber piles in a shallow turquoise lagoon behind a sandbar: long houses with tall
saddle-shaped shingle roofs that sweep up to points, linked by curved boardwalks around round water

**Prompt:**
> Concept art of an invented Age of Sail culture town (not based on any real-world culture): the Lagoon-Weaver people, who build on a shallow turquoise lagoon behind a sandbar. The whole town stands on dark tarred timber piles and woven platforms joined by curved boardwalks and arched footbridges; long houses with tall, steep, boat-hull-shaped roofs whose ridges sweep up into points at both ends, roofed in silver-grey wooden shingles with moss-green patches; houses grouped in round clusters around open circular water courts; floating garden rafts of vegetables; a tall round pier-temple with a spiral roof at the centre; channels full of narrow boats and a harbour of two-masted ships with large fan-shaped ribbed sails in saffron and cream; a long woven breakwater; mangrove-like trees on the shore. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows and sparkling on the water, high oblique aerial view from about 600 metres away looking down at 40 degrees, the whole town readable from above with clear roofs, boardwalks and channels, soft atmospheric haze, rich natural colours, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

**For the 3D artist:**
- Silhouette: steep saddle roofs with upswept points, silver-grey shingle with moss; the roofs are
  most of what the camera sees, so they carry the culture.
- Layout is circular and organic: clusters of houses around round water courts, linked by curving
  boardwalks; no straight streets at all.
- Palette: turquoise water, grey-silver wood, moss green, saffron sails. Floating green garden rafts
  scatter colour across the water.
- Built entirely over water: piles, platforms and a woven breakwater; this culture favours coastal
  lagoons and its `port` is the whole town.
- Caution: the generated upswept roofs and ribbed sails sit close to some real-world vernacular
  forms. For the final asset, push them further (e.g. asymmetric ridges, woven-panel sails) so the
  culture stays clearly invented.

Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, golden-hour low sun from the left casting long warm shadows, high oblique aerial view from about 600 metres away looking down at 45 degrees, the whole town readable from above with clear roofs, radial streets, walls and fields, soft atmospheric haze, rich natural colours, crisp detail, physically based lighting. No text, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

**For the 3D artist:**
- The layout is the identity: concentric rings + radial spokes + sunburst fields. Procedural town

- Signature landmark: the central spire of stacked, narrowing stone discs.
- Palette: cool pale stone and blue-grey slate on olive moorland with purple heather; the coolest
  of the three cultures, a good contrast with A (warm) and B (aqua).

## city-night.png — Atomic age metropolis at night

The same metropolis as `age-5-atomic.png`, from the same camera, at night: dark city with warm
lit windows, amber highway ribbons with car light trails, a floodlit domed temple, lamp-lined
bridges reflecting in the river, lit port cranes and red warning lights on the cooling towers.

**Prompt:**
> The same metropolis as the reference image, same camera angle and framing, but at night under a clear starry sky with a faint deep-blue afterglow on the horizon: the city is dark navy and black with thousands of warm sodium-orange and soft white lit windows, the lights densest in the old core around the floodlit domed temple and thinning out toward the suburbs on the hills, highways drawn as bright continuous ribbons of amber streetlights with red and white car-light streaks, bridges outlined by lamp chains reflected in the dark river, the port lit by white floodlights on the cranes, the cooling towers with red aircraft-warning lights and pale steam lit from below, dark parks and fields as unlit gaps so the street grid reads clearly from above. Cinematic near-photoreal concept art for a god game, high oblique aerial view from about 1 kilometre away looking down at 40 degrees, rich contrast, crisp detail, physically based lighting, light glow but no blown-out haze. No text, no signs, no logos, no labels, no watermark, no UI.

*(Generated with `age-5-atomic.png` as the reference image.)*

**For the 3D artist / lighting:**
- Lights follow structure: highways and main streets are continuous amber lines; blocks are
  speckled with small warm window lights; parks, fields and water stay dark. That contrast is what
  makes a city read from above (and from orbit, as in `shots/ue-cloudlab-terminator-final.png`).
- Two colour temperatures only: sodium orange (streets, most windows) and a cooler white (towers,
  port floodlights). A few red points (warning lights) as accents.
- The landmark (domed temple) is floodlit warm from below so it remains the focal point at night.
- Lit steam over the cooling towers and lamp reflections on the river add depth; keep bloom subtle.

## Notes for the lead

- Camera distance drifts a little across the set (the culture shots are a bit closer, around
  300–500 m; the late ages are around 1 km) because the generator interprets distance loosely.
- Known gaps against the prompts: Sail-age walls stayed round instead of star-shaped; the Space-age
  launch site is small and far back in frame. Both are noted under the images above.
